Disney Believe: What We Know About Disney's Next Cruise Ship

By Ryan · November 23, 2026

The short answer

The Disney Believe is Disney Cruise Line's next ship and the last of the Wish class. Disney says its maiden voyage is December 18, 2027. Announced highlights include the first-ever stage version of Encanto, a Moana-inspired Grand Hall, Oaken's Table (a Frozen family feast), a Snow White lounge called Wishing Well, and Sea Notes, a Little Mermaid piano bar.

Disney Cruise Line's next ship has a name, a date and a growing list of things to look forward to. Disney keeps a running log of Disney Believe news, and the latest updates fill in a lot of the picture. Here's everything announced so far, in plain English.

When does the Disney Believe sail?

Disney has set the maiden voyage for December 18, 2027. The ship is being built at the Meyer Werft shipyard in Papenburg, Germany, and Disney says it's the final ship of the Wish class, the same family as the Disney Wish, Disney Treasure and Disney Destiny.

Construction is moving along. Disney says the first of three giant hull sections, the middle of the ship with the Grand Hall, floated out in September 2026, and the keel was laid in October with a Tinker Bell coin placed underneath for good luck (a shipbuilding tradition).

What will be on board?

Here's what Disney has revealed so far:

  • Encanto on stage. The Walt Disney Theatre will host the first-ever stage version of Encanto. That's a big one for families who have the soundtrack on repeat.
  • A Moana-inspired Grand Hall, the ship's big central atrium.
  • Oaken's Table: A Frozen Family Feast, a new take on Frozen-themed dining.
  • Wishing Well, a storybook lounge inspired by Snow White.
  • Sea Notes, a Little Mermaid piano bar with a live pianist, Flounder and Sebastian sculptures and an ocean-colored design.
  • Spaces themed to Inside Out 2, Big Hero 6 and Zootopia.
  • On the outside: Woody and Buzz Lightyear on the stern, and Tinker Bell artwork painted on the bow.

Disney has also named the ship's opening Cruise Director, Trent Hitchcock.

Should you book a brand-new ship for your first cruise?

It's tempting, and there's a lot to love about being among the first on board. Here's how we'd weigh it:

  • The upside: everything is new, and you'll get to see shows and spaces nobody else has.
  • The trade-offs: maiden season sailings are in high demand, and new ships sometimes adjust things in the first few months. Compare fares with a sailing on an older ship before you decide.
  • The middle path: if you love the Wish-class look but want more choice now, the Disney Wish is sailing today and shares a lot of the same design ideas. Our ship comparison lays out the differences.

Where will the Disney Believe sail?

Disney's Believe announcements focus on the ship itself rather than where it goes. Our Disney Believe page tracks the home port and first itineraries as Disney confirms them, and we'll update it as soon as there's more to share.

Quick questions

When does the Disney Believe start sailing?

Disney has announced a maiden voyage date of December 18, 2027.

What class of ship is the Disney Believe?

Disney describes it as the final ship of the Wish class, built at the Meyer Werft shipyard in Papenburg, Germany.

What's new on the Disney Believe?

Announced highlights include the first stage adaptation of Encanto, a Moana-inspired Grand Hall, Oaken's Table: A Frozen Family Feast, the Wishing Well lounge, the Sea Notes piano bar, and spaces themed to Inside Out 2, Big Hero 6 and Zootopia.

How much does a Disney Believe cruise cost?

Disney hasn't listed prices in its Disney Believe announcements. Check Disney Cruise Line's site for current sailings and fares.
