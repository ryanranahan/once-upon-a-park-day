Disney Cruise Line Fall 2027 and Spring 2028: Where Every Ship Sails

By Ryan · November 23, 2026

The short answer

Disney Cruise Line's fall 2027 and spring 2028 sailings are open to book. Highlights: the Disney Wish sails from New York in fall 2027 (including Bermuda and New England and Canada trips), the Disney Destiny gets its first Port Canaveral season, the Disney Magic makes two 14-night Panama Canal crossings, and Disney Fantasy adds its first 8-night Bermuda trip from Fort Lauderdale.

Disney announced its fall 2027 and spring 2028 sailings back in late summer, and they're open to book now. There's a lot packed into the announcement, so we've boiled it down to what each ship is doing and who each trip suits.

What are the big headlines?

  • The Disney Wish sails from New York. After its first European season, Disney says the Wish arrives in New York in late September 2027 for seven sailings of 4 to 7 nights. Five go to Bermuda, and there are New England and Atlantic Canada trips too, the first from New York in nearly four years.
  • The Disney Magic crosses the Panama Canal. Two 14-night repositioning trips, with first-ever stops at Fuerte Amador (Panama City) heading east and Colón heading west.
  • The Disney Destiny moves to Port Canaveral for the first time, with 3- to 7-night Bahamas and Western Caribbean trips after opening the season in Fort Lauderdale.
  • The Disney Fantasy gets its first 8-night Bermuda trip from Fort Lauderdale.

Where is each ship sailing?

Ship Fall 2027 and spring 2028 plan (per Disney)
Disney Wish New York in fall, then San Juan, then back to Port Canaveral for 3- and 4-night Bahamas trips to Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay
Disney Destiny Fort Lauderdale, then its first Port Canaveral season: 3- to 7-night Bahamas and Western Caribbean
Disney Treasure 7-night Eastern and Western Caribbean, plus an 8-night Eastern Caribbean and a 6-night Bahamas trip with two Castaway Cay days
Disney Dream 3- and 4-night Bahamas from Port Canaveral, then Fort Lauderdale from November 2027 for 3- to 5-night Bahamas. One 6-night trip with two Castaway Cay days
Disney Fantasy Port Canaveral (4 and 5 nights) September to November 2027, then Fort Lauderdale for 4- to 7-night trips and an 8-night Bermuda sailing
Disney Magic Two 14-night Panama Canal crossings, then Galveston for 4- to 7-night trips, including select 7-night Bahamas trips
Disney Wonder San Diego: 3- to 7-night Baja and Mexican Riviera (Ensenada, Cabo San Lucas, Catalina Island)
Disney Adventure Singapore: 3- and 4-night sailings, September to December 2027

Disney also lists 5- to 7-night Southern and Eastern Caribbean trips from San Juan on several ships during the season. Halloween on the High Seas starts in September 2027, and Very Merrytime sailings run in November and December 2027.

Which of these trips suits a first-timer?

Here's how we'd sort them:

  • Easiest first cruise: a 3- or 4-night Bahamas trip from Port Canaveral on the Wish, Destiny or Dream. Short, simple, and close to Walt Disney World if you want to add park days.
  • Live in the Northeast? The Wish from New York means no flight to Florida. Bermuda in particular makes a great 5-night trip.
  • Live in Texas or the middle of the country? The Magic from Galveston is a drive-to option.
  • West Coast? The Wonder from San Diego is the easy pick.
  • Bucket-list trip? The Magic's Panama Canal crossings are long and port-heavy, so they suit experienced cruisers or anyone with time to spare. Remember they're one-way, so plan your flights.

How do you book?

General booking opened September 8, 2026, on Disney Cruise Line's website, after an earlier window for returning guests in the Castaway Club. Popular dates, especially holidays and the New York sailings, can fill fast, so if one of these jumped out at you, it's worth checking now.

Quick questions

Is the Disney Wish sailing from New York in 2027?

Yes. Disney says the Wish arrives in New York in late September 2027 after its first European season, for seven sailings of 4 to 7 nights, including Bermuda and New England and Canada trips.

Which Disney ship goes through the Panama Canal in 2027?

The Disney Magic, on two 14-night repositioning sailings. Disney says they include first-ever calls at Fuerte Amador (Panama City) and Colón.

Where does the Disney Destiny sail in fall 2027?

It opens the season in Fort Lauderdale, then moves to Port Canaveral for the first time, sailing 3- to 7-night Bahamas and Western Caribbean trips.

Are these sailings open for booking?

Yes. General booking opened September 8, 2026, on Disney Cruise Line's website, after an early window for Castaway Club members.

Where these facts come from

We checked the details in this post against Disney's own announcements on November 23, 2026. Dates, menus and sailings change, so confirm on Disney's site before you book.
