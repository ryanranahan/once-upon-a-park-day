Holidays at Disneyland 2026: Dates, Highlights and a Hotel Offer

By Ryan · November 23, 2026

The short answer

The holiday season at the Disneyland Resort runs November 13, 2026, through January 6, 2027. Highlights include Sleeping Beauty's Winter Castle, "it's a small world" Holiday, World of Color - Season of Light, the Disney Festival of Holidays at Disney California Adventure, and Santa in both parks and all three Disneyland Resort hotels. Disney also has a hotel offer of up to 20% off select 3-night-plus stays, booked by December 15, 2026.

The holidays are one of the prettiest times to visit the Disneyland Resort in California, and the season is already underway. Disney shared its full holiday guide, so here are the highlights for first-timers, plus a hotel offer with a deadline coming up.

When is the holiday season at Disneyland?

It runs November 13, 2026, through January 6, 2027, across both Disneyland park and Disney California Adventure.

What are the highlights?

At Disneyland park

  • Sleeping Beauty's Winter Castle, covered in snowy decor and, Disney says, about 80,000 lights.
  • "it's a small world" Holiday, with around 400,000 lights on the outside. It's lovely after dark.
- **Santa** has a new spot this year at **Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn**. - **Chip 'n' Dale's Ornament Trail** runs November 11 through January 6.

At Disney California Adventure

  • Disney Festival of Holidays, with food booths celebrating holiday traditions from around the world, plus Disney ¡Viva Navidad!, the festival's Latin holiday celebration.
  • World of Color - Season of Light, the holiday version of the nighttime water show.
  • A Sip and Savor Pass for the festival (six digital food coupons) is sold separately if you plan to graze.

At the hotels

Santa also visits all three Disneyland Resort hotels, and holiday character dining at the hotels runs November 16 through January 3. Disney said reservations would open on Disneyland.com, so check there if it's on your list.

Is there a holiday hotel deal?

Yes. Disney's offer is up to 20% off select stays of 3 nights or more at the Disneyland Hotel, the Grand Californian, Pixar Place and The Villas at Disneyland Hotel.

The fine print that matters:

  • Book by December 15, 2026.
  • Stays from October 11 through December 18, 2026, with travel finished by December 19.
  • It's subject to availability, so not every room or date will qualify.

If you've been on the fence about a December trip, this is the window. Our Disneyland Resort hotels page explains the differences between the three hotels in plain English.

How should first-timers plan a holiday Disneyland trip?

  • Go on a weekday if you can. Weekends in December are busy with locals.
  • See the lights at night. The castle and "it's a small world" are both at their best after sunset, so save a little energy for the evening.
  • Plan one park per day for the evenings. World of Color is in California Adventure and the castle lighting is in Disneyland, so split your nights.
  • Graze the festival for lunch. The Festival of Holidays booths are an easy way to try a lot without a sit-down meal.
  • Book character dining as soon as it opens. Holiday reservations go quickly.

Quick questions

When are the holidays at Disneyland in 2026?

November 13, 2026, through January 6, 2027, according to Disney.

Where is Santa at Disneyland in 2026?

Disney says Santa appears at Disneyland park, Disney California Adventure and the three Disneyland Resort hotels. At Disneyland park, his spot moves to Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn this year.

Is there a Disneyland hotel deal for the holidays?

Disney's offer is up to 20% off select stays of 3 nights or more at the Disneyland Hotel, Grand Californian, Pixar Place and The Villas at Disneyland Hotel. Book by December 15, 2026, for stays through December 18, 2026, finishing by December 19. Availability is limited.

Is the Disney Festival of Holidays included with a park ticket?

The festival is part of a regular Disney California Adventure day. Food is paid for as you go, and a Sip and Savor Pass with six coupons is sold separately.

Where these facts come from

We checked the details in this post against Disney's own announcements on November 23, 2026. Dates, menus and sailings change, so confirm on Disney's site before you book.
