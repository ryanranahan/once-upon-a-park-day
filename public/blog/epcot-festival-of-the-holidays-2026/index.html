EPCOT Festival of the Holidays 2026: A First-Timer's Guide

By Ryan · November 23, 2026

The short answer

The 2026 EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays runs November 27 through December 30, 2026. It's included with regular EPCOT admission, and food and drinks are paid for as you go. Disney says there are 20 Holiday Kitchens serving 186 items, 98 of them new, plus the Holiday Cookie Stroll and the Candlelight Processional.

If you're heading to Walt Disney World between Thanksgiving and New Year's, EPCOT is about to become the coziest park on property. Disney has shared the menu for the 2026 EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays, and we read through all of it so you don't have to. Here's what matters if it's your first time.

When is the festival, and what does it cost?

The festival runs November 27 through December 30, 2026. There's no extra festival ticket to buy. You need a normal EPCOT ticket (and a park reservation if your ticket type needs one). The food and drinks are sold one item at a time, so you can try two bites or twenty.

What's actually there?

Disney says this year's festival has 186 food and drink offerings across 20 Holiday Kitchens, and 98 of them are new. Most of the kitchens are set up around World Showcase, and each one leans on a holiday tradition from a different part of the world.

A few new things caught our eye:

  • A new Diwali Holiday Kitchen, which is a lovely addition to the festival's around-the-world idea.
  • Season's Eatings, a new kitchen at CommuniCore Plaza. The headliner is a Holly Jolly Bánh Mì.
  • The Prototype Cookie at CommuniCore Hall, from the playfully named Experimental Prototype Cookies of Tomorrow.
  • A Hot Cocoa Funnel Cake at The American Adventure, which is exactly the kind of thing we'd split three ways.

How does the Holiday Cookie Stroll work?

This is the festival's version of a scavenger hunt, and it's an easy win with kids. Look for menu items marked with a cookie icon. Each one you buy earns a stamp in your Festival Passport (the free festival guide). Once you have five stamps, take the passport to Holiday Sweets & Treats and you'll get a special sweet as your reward.

Our tip: don't try to do all five in one afternoon unless you have a sweet tooth to match. Spread the stamps over the day, and share each cookie so everyone gets a taste without a sugar crash before dinner.

What else is there besides food?

The entertainment is a big part of the charm. Disney lists the Holiday Storytellers around World Showcase, the Voices of Liberty and Dickens Carolers, the show JOYFUL! A Celebration of the Season, and the Candlelight Processional, a choir-and-orchestra retelling of the Christmas story with a celebrity narrator.

There's also a new PhotoPass Magic Shot. Look for the photographer holding a cookie-tray prop near the big Christmas tree at the World Showcase entrance.

How should a first-timer plan an EPCOT holiday day?

Here's the simple plan we'd suggest:

  1. Ride first, eat later. Do the big rides in the morning while World Showcase is still quiet. The Holiday Kitchens get busiest from mid-afternoon on.
  2. Pick up a Festival Passport early. It doubles as a map of every kitchen and every cookie stop.
  3. Graze instead of booking a big dinner. Three or four festival plates per person usually adds up to a meal.
  4. Plan on weekends being busy. The weeks around Christmas are some of the busiest of the year, so weekday visits earlier in December are usually calmer.
  5. Sit down for the Candlelight Processional if you can. Seats fill up, so get there early for the show time you want.

Where should you stay for an EPCOT-heavy trip?

If you think you'll want to come back to EPCOT more than once, staying close helps a lot. The Beach Club, Yacht Club and BoardWalk Inn are all an easy walk to EPCOT's World Showcase entrance, so you can pop back to the room for a break and walk back for the evening lights. Quick questions

When is the 2026 EPCOT Festival of the Holidays?

It runs November 27 through December 30, 2026, according to Disney's announcement.

Do you need a separate ticket for the Festival of the Holidays?

No. Disney hasn't announced a separate festival ticket. You need a regular EPCOT park ticket and a park reservation if your ticket requires one, and you pay for festival food and drinks as you go.

What is the Holiday Cookie Stroll?

Buy five items marked with the cookie icon to fill the stamps in your Festival Passport, then turn in the finished passport at Holiday Sweets & Treats for a special sweet.

What's new at the 2026 festival?

Disney lists a new Diwali Holiday Kitchen, a new Season's Eatings kitchen at CommuniCore Plaza with a Holly Jolly Bánh Mì, and a new Prototype Cookie at CommuniCore Hall.

Where these facts come from

We checked the details in this post against Disney's own announcements on November 23, 2026. Dates, menus and sailings change, so confirm on Disney's site before you book.
