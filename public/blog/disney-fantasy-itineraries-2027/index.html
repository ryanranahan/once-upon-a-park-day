Disney Fantasy Itineraries for 2027: How to Pick the Right Sailing

By Ryan · November 23, 2026

The short answer

In 2027 the Disney Fantasy sails 3-, 4- and 5-night Bahamas and Caribbean trips, a 6-night one-way trip from San Juan to Port Canaveral, and a special 10-night Southern Caribbean voyage from Port Canaveral in summer 2027. Theme sailings include Pixar Day at Sea (select 5-night trips, January to April), Halloween on the High Seas (September and October) and Very Merrytime (November and December).

The Disney Fantasy is a ship we know firsthand, so we were happy to see Disney put out a full rundown of where she's sailing in 2027. If you're comparing Fantasy sailings, here's how they break down and how we'd choose. (For the ship itself, see our Disney Fantasy guide.)

Where does the Disney Fantasy sail in 2027?

The Fantasy sails three kinds of trips: the Bahamas, the Western Caribbean and the Eastern Caribbean. Ports Disney lists include Nassau, Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point (Disney's two private destinations), Cozumel, St. Thomas and Philipsburg on St. Maarten. Some Eastern Caribbean trips are one-way, linking Port Canaveral, San Juan and Fort Lauderdale.

How long are the sailings?

Length What it is
3 nights Bahamas from Port Canaveral, with a Castaway Cay stop
4 nights Bahamas from Port Canaveral or Fort Lauderdale
5 nights Bahamas, Western Caribbean or Eastern Caribbean
6 nights One-way from San Juan to Port Canaveral, via Philipsburg, St. Thomas, Lookout Cay and Castaway Cay
10 nights Southern Caribbean from Port Canaveral, summer 2027 only

The 10-night Southern Caribbean trip

This is the standout. Disney says it visits Philipsburg, St. Lucia, Antigua and San Juan, plus both Lookout Cay and Castaway Cay. It's a lot of islands in one trip, and it's a rare chance to go farther south from Florida on a Disney ship.

What theme sailings does the Fantasy have?

  • Pixar Day at Sea: select 5-night sailings from January through April 2027, with Pixar characters, pool parties and themed dining.
  • Halloween on the High Seas: select sailings in September and October 2027, with trick-or-treating and a Halloween party.
  • Very Merrytime: select sailings in November and December. Our Very Merrytime guide explains what's included.

Which Fantasy sailing is best for first-timers?

Here's our take on how to choose:

  • Trying cruising for the first time? A 3- or 4-night Bahamas trip is the easy yes. You get a Castaway Cay day, a taste of the shows and dining, and less money on the line.
  • Want the full Fantasy experience? Go for 5 nights or more. The Fantasy is built for longer trips, and you'll have time for the shows, the AquaDuck and a slow sea day.
  • Traveling with kids in the spring? Look at the Pixar Day at Sea sailings.
  • Have a big trip to celebrate? The 10-night Southern Caribbean trip is the one to watch. Expect it to be popular.
  • One-way trips need extra planning. A San Juan to Port Canaveral trip means booking a one-way flight, so add that into your cost before you compare.

What's on board the Disney Fantasy?

The quick version: three rotational dining rooms (Royal Court, Enchanted Garden and Animator's Palate), adults-only Palo and Remy, the AquaDuck water coaster, kids clubs for every age, and Broadway-style shows including Disney's Aladdin, Disney's Believe and Frozen, A Musical Spectacular. Our ship guide has the full picture, and our ship comparison shows how she stacks up against the rest of the fleet.

Quick questions

Where does the Disney Fantasy sail in 2027?

Bahamas, Western Caribbean and Eastern Caribbean trips, with ports such as Nassau, Castaway Cay, Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point, Cozumel, St. Thomas and Philipsburg. Some Eastern Caribbean trips are one-way between Port Canaveral, San Juan and Fort Lauderdale.

How long are Disney Fantasy cruises in 2027?

Disney lists 3-, 4-, 5-, 6- and 10-night sailings. The 10-night trip is a Southern Caribbean voyage from Port Canaveral in summer 2027.

Does the Disney Fantasy have Pixar Day at Sea in 2027?

Yes, on select 5-night sailings from January through April 2027, according to Disney.

Is the Disney Fantasy good for a first cruise?

Yes. It has the AquaDuck water coaster, three rotational dining rooms, big kids clubs and adults-only spaces, and its shorter 3- and 4-night trips are an easy first taste of cruising.

Where these facts come from

We checked the details in this post against Disney's own announcements on November 23, 2026. Dates, menus and sailings change, so confirm on Disney's site before you book.
