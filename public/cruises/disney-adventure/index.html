Overview and who it suits

Disney Adventure is Disney's biggest ship by far, and the first based in Asia. It sails short trips from Singapore and is built like a floating theme park, with seven themed areas, including Marvel Landing, Toy Story Place, San Fransokyo Street and Wayfinder Bay.

Pools and water features

Signature water ride: Woody and Jessie's Wild Slides, plus the Ironcycle Test Run roller coaster

Toy Story Place has Woody and Jessie's Wild Slides, and Marvel Landing has the Ironcycle Test Run, Disney's first roller coaster at sea, plus Pym Quantum Racers and Groot Galaxy Spin.

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • Disney's first roller coaster at sea
  • Seven themed areas to explore
  • Short trips that are easy to add to an Asia vacation

Keep in mind

  • Mostly sea days, with Langkawi on select sailings
  • It's a very big ship

First-timer tips

  • Look for the 4-night sailings that stop in Langkawi, Malaysia, between December 31, 2026 and May 2027.
  • Plan your Marvel Landing time early in the trip.
