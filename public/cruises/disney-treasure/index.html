Overview and who it suits

Disney Treasure is the Wish's sister ship, themed to adventure and exploration. It sails week-long Caribbean trips from Port Canaveral, which makes it the pick for families who want a full week at sea.

Quick answers

How big is the Disney Treasure?
The Disney Treasure is about 144,000 gross tons, with 1,250 staterooms and 15 decks. It carries about 2,500 guests at two per room, up to about 4,000.
Where does the Disney Treasure sail from?
The Disney Treasure sails from Port Canaveral.
What are the restaurants on the Disney Treasure?
The main dining rotation on the Disney Treasure is Plaza de Coco, Worlds of Marvel and 1923. You move between them on different nights, and your servers move with you. Adult-only dining, for an extra charge, is Palo Steakhouse and Enchanté.
What are the downsides of the Disney Treasure?
Things to keep in mind: Mostly 7-night sailings, so it's a bigger time commitment. It's a big ship, so plan where you're going.
What should first-time cruisers know about the Disney Treasure?
If a week feels long, look for the 6-night Bahamian sailing with two days at Castaway Cay. Book Palo Steakhouse or Enchanté for a sea day.
