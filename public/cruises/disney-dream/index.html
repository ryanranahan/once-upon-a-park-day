Overview and who it suits

Disney Dream was the first of Disney's bigger ships. It introduced the AquaDuck water coaster and the virtual portholes in inside staterooms, and it specializes in short Bahamian cruises, which makes it one of the best first-cruise ships in the fleet.

Pools and water features

Signature water ride: AquaDuck water coaster

The AquaDuck water coaster loops around the top deck and out over the side of the ship. Families have Mickey's Pool and Donald's Pool, little ones have Nemo's Reef, and the Funnel Vision screen shows movies by the pool. Adults get Quiet Cove and Satellite Falls.

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • The AquaDuck
  • Short sailings that are easy to fit in
  • Virtual portholes make inside rooms feel bigger

Keep in mind

  • Short trips can feel busy on the pool deck
  • Fewer port days on the 3-night sailings

First-timer tips

  • Ride the AquaDuck on embarkation day or during port days, when the line is shorter.
  • Look for the 6-night sailing with two days at Castaway Cay in the 2027 to 2028 season.
