Your first Disney cruise: what's included and what costs extra

A Disney cruise covers more than most first-timers expect. Here's what's in the fare, what isn't, and the dates to put on your calendar.

Researched guide Facts last checked November 2026

Included in your fare

Disney's terms say all meals and entertainment on board are included. In practice that means:

  • Main dining in the rotational restaurants, plus buffet and quick-service food
  • Room service on most ships
  • Soft drinks with meals and at the drink stations
  • Kids clubs for ages 3 to 17
  • Stage shows, movies, deck parties and character meet-and-greets
  • Pools and water attractions
  • Food and soft drinks at Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point

What costs extra

  • Gratuities. These are added to your onboard account automatically based on how long you sail. You can adjust them at Guest Services.
  • Alcohol, specialty coffee, bottled water and drinks ordered at the bars
  • Adult dining at Palo, Remy and Enchanté
  • Port adventures (shore excursions)
  • Spa services
  • Wi-Fi, photos and the Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique
  • The nursery for babies and toddlers
  • Laundry

Disney also adds an automatic service charge to spa services and bar or drink tabs, so check your receipts.

When first-timers can book

Activities, port adventures, Palo, Remy and spa bookings open in order of Castaway Club level. As a first-time cruiser, your window opens last, about 75 days before you sail. Set a reminder and book the things you care about most on day one.

You'll also do online check-in before you sail. Repeat cruisers get first pick of arrival times, so check in as soon as your window opens.

First-timer tips

  • Pick a short sailing first. A 3- or 4-night Bahamas cruise is an easy way to find out if you love it.
  • Book one adult dinner at Palo or Remy if you can. It's a highlight for many couples.
  • Plan your beach day. Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay are included, so you don't need a port adventure to have a great day.
  • Pack a small day bag. Your room may not be ready right away on boarding day.
