Overview and who it suits

Disney Fantasy is the Dream's sister ship, with the same AquaDuck and virtual portholes, but it's set up for longer trips. It's known for the Caribbean, and it had a dry dock in fall 2025 that added a new concierge tower suite and updated the spa and concierge lounge.

Early to mid-December 2027

Southern and Eastern Caribbean

From San Juan, Puerto Rico · Length 5 to 7 nights

  • A 7-night Southern Caribbean Very Merrytime sailing on December 3

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Starting in San Juan puts the Southern Caribbean within reach, with islands you can't easily get to on a short Florida cruise.

Mid-December 2027 to April 2028

The Bahamas and Eastern Caribbean

From Fort Lauderdale, Florida · Length 4 to 7 nights

  • Disney's first-ever Bermuda cruise: 8 nights, visiting both Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay (March 18, 2028)

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

The Bermuda sailing is a true first for Disney, with pink-sand beaches plus both Bahamian island stops. A great one for repeat cruisers.

Late April to May 2028

Southern and Eastern Caribbean

From San Juan, then back to Port Canaveral · Length 6 and 7 nights

  • Moves to San Juan on April 23, then returns to Port Canaveral on May 13

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

These one-way sailings are a fun way to see more of the Caribbean in one trip.

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • Longer itineraries with more ports
  • The AquaDuck
  • Fresh updates from the 2025 dry dock

Keep in mind

  • Longer trips cost more
  • Sea days can make the pool deck busy

First-timer tips

  • The 8-night Bermuda sailing visits both Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay, so it's worth watching for.
  • On longer trips, book a Palo or Remy night early in the cruise.
