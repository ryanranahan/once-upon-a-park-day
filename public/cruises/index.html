Which Disney ship is right for you?

All 9 ships in the fleet, from the original Disney Magic to the newest. Start with the comparison, then dig into each ship.

The fleet

Magic class

Since 1998 · We've sailed her Disney Magic Disney's original ship, with a classic Art Deco look and a cozier size than the newer ships. Since 1999 Disney Wonder The Magic's sister ship, with an Art Nouveau look and the same classic, smaller-ship feel.

Dream class

Since 2011 · We've sailed her Disney Dream The first of the larger ships, which introduced the AquaDuck water coaster and virtual portholes in inside staterooms. Since 2012 · We've sailed her Disney Fantasy The Dream's sister ship, with an Art Nouveau look, often sailing longer Caribbean itineraries.

Wish class

Since 2022 Disney Wish The first of the newest generation of ships, with a grand fairy-tale atrium and new themed dining. Since 2024 Disney Treasure The Wish's sister ship, themed around adventure and exploration. Since 2025 Disney Destiny Disney's heroes-and-villains ship and the third in the Wish class, launched in late 2025.

Global class

Since 2026 Disney Adventure Disney's largest ship, built for 3- and 4-night sailings from Singapore.

Coming soon

Debuts 2027 Disney Believe Disney's next ship, scheduled to sail its maiden voyage in December 2027 from Fort Lauderdale.
