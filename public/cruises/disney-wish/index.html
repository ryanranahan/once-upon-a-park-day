Overview and who it suits

Disney Wish was the first of Disney's newest generation of ships. It brought the AquaMouse, the first Disney attraction at sea, and a fresh mix of themed dining, and in 2027 it heads to Europe and New York for the first time.

Kids, teens and adults

Kids and teens

  • It's a Small World Nursery (6 months to 3 years)
  • Oceaneer Club (ages 3 to 10)
  • Edge (ages 11 to 14)
  • Vibe (ages 14 to 17)
  • The Hideaway, a late-night space for ages 18 to 20

Adults

  • Palo Steakhouse and Enchanté, adults-only dining (extra cost)
  • The Rose, Nightingale's, The Bayou and Keg & Compass
  • An adults-only pool and bar on the upper deck
  • Senses Spa

Pools and water features

Signature water ride: AquaMouse water ride

The AquaMouse is a water ride with show scenes and about 760 feet of tubes. Little ones get the Toy Story Splash Zone, and there are about 10 pools and water-play areas across the ship.

Shows and entertainment

Recent stage shows in the main theater include:

  • The Little Mermaid
  • Disney Seas the Adventure
  • Disney's Aladdin

Show lineups change, so check the Navigator app for your sailing.

Transatlantic

September 17, 2027

Transatlantic

From Southampton to New York · Length 12 nights

  • Stops in Scotland, Iceland and Canada

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

A northern crossing with real variety in its ports, which is rare for a transatlantic trip.

Late September to October 2027

Bermuda, New England and Atlantic Canada

From New York · Length 4 to 7 nights

  • Seven sailings, five of them to Bermuda
  • Three Bermuda sailings add an extra day in port
  • Most October sailings are Halloween on the High Seas

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Disney's first New York season since 2023. Fall foliage, Bermuda beaches and Halloween at sea make this a fantastic pick for families in the Northeast.

November 2027

Southern and Eastern Caribbean

From San Juan, Puerto Rico · Length 4 to 7 nights

  • A 7-night Southern Caribbean sailing on November 5
  • A 4-night sailing from San Juan to Port Canaveral on November 12, stopping at Lookout Cay and Castaway Cay

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Southern Caribbean islands on the Wish are a rare combination, so this short window is worth a look.

Mid-November 2027 to spring 2028

The Bahamas

From Port Canaveral, Florida · Length 3 and 4 nights

  • Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point
  • Very Merrytime holiday sailings in November and December

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Back to the classic short Bahamian trips, now with holiday sailings. Easy to fit around a Walt Disney World visit.

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • The AquaMouse
  • Arendelle: A Frozen Dining Adventure
  • Big, modern kids clubs

Keep in mind

  • Short Bahamian trips go fast
  • It's a big ship, so plan where you're going

First-timer tips

  • Book Arendelle as early as you can if your kids love Frozen.
  • The fall 2027 New York season is a rare chance to cruise Disney from the Northeast.
