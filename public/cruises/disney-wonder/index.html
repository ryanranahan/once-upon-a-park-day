Overview and who it suits

Disney Wonder is the Magic's twin, with an Art Nouveau look and the same easy-to-navigate size. It's Disney's West Coast ship, sailing from San Diego to Baja and the Mexican Riviera, with Alaska in the summer.

Pools and water features

Signature water ride: AquaLab splash zone

The AquaLab splash area has the Twist 'n' Spout slide, and Dory's Reef is a play area for the littlest swimmers. There are three pools, including Goofy's Pool and the adults-only Quiet Cove.

Shows and entertainment

Recent stage shows in the main theater include:

  • Frozen, A Musical Spectacular
  • Disney Dreams: An Enchanted Classic
  • The Golden Mickeys

Show lineups change, so check the Navigator app for your sailing.

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • The easiest way to cruise Disney from the West Coast
  • Short, affordable Baja sailings
  • Alaska in the summer

Keep in mind

  • Fewer big water attractions than the newer ships
  • Fewer verandah rooms than the larger ships

First-timer tips

  • Tiana's Place is one of the rotational restaurants; ask about the live music night.
  • If you're flying in, San Diego makes it easy to add a day at the beach or the zoo before you board.
