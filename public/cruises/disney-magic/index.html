Overview and who it suits

Disney Magic is where Disney Cruise Line began, and it still has the classic ocean-liner look. It's smaller than the newer ships, which makes it easy to find your way around, and in 2027 and 2028 it covers more ground than any other ship in the fleet: the Caribbean from Galveston, Mexico from San Diego, Alaska, and the Panama Canal.

Pools and water features

Signature water ride: AquaDunk drop slide

The AquaDunk drop slide shoots you out over the side of the ship. There's also the AquaLab splash area for kids, a family pool, and the Quiet Cove pool for adults.

Itineraries and ports

Where the Disney Magic is scheduled to sail, from now through Disney's latest published schedule. Dates and ports can change, so always check Disney's site before you book.

Now through April 2027

Western Caribbean and the Bahamas

From Galveston, Texas · Length 4 to 7 nights

  • Cozumel and Progreso, Mexico
  • Select sailings reach the Bahamas

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Galveston is a great drive-to option for families in Texas and the middle of the country, and the Western Caribbean ports are full of beaches, ruins and easy excursions.

April 25, 2027

Panama Canal

From Galveston to San Diego · Length 14 nights

  • A full transit of the Panama Canal

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

A once-in-a-lifetime kind of trip. Two weeks at sea with Disney service, and a day going through the locks that you'll talk about for years.

Dates from a fan schedule, not yet confirmed by Disney.

May to September 2027

Alaska

From Vancouver, Canada · Length 6 to 8 nights

  • Glaciers, wildlife and Alaska's Inside Passage
  • Repositioning sailings from San Diego in May and back in September

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Alaska on a Disney ship is a special combination: cozy, scenic sea days and some of the most dramatic scenery anywhere. A great pick for families with older kids.

Late September to early November 2027

Baja and the Mexican Riviera

From San Diego, California · Length 3 to 7 nights

  • Ensenada, Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Vallarta, Mazatlán and Catalina Island

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Short Baja trips are a perfect first cruise from the West Coast, and the longer Mexican Riviera sailings add some of Mexico's prettiest beach towns.

November 4, 2027

Panama Canal

From San Diego to Galveston · Length 14 nights

  • Disney's first visit to Fuerte Amador, Panama

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

The eastbound crossing adds a brand-new port for Disney, which makes it a fun one for repeat cruisers who want something new.

Mid-November 2027 to March 2028

Western Caribbean and the Bahamas

From Galveston, Texas · Length 4 to 7 nights

  • Progreso, Cozumel and Nassau
  • Select 7-night Bahamian sailings stop at both Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point
  • Very Merrytime holiday sailings in November and December

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Getting both of Disney's Bahamian island stops on one trip from Texas is a real treat, and the holiday sailings bring a lot of extra sparkle.

March 26, 2028

Panama Canal

From Galveston to San Diego · Length 14 nights

  • Disney's first visit to Colón, Panama

Why it's worth a look Draft for Ryan

Another brand-new Disney port, and a springtime way to see the canal.

April 2028

Baja, the Mexican Riviera and the Pacific Coast

From San Diego to Vancouver · Length 5 to 7 nights, then a 4-night coastal sailing

  • A Pacific Coast repositioning sailing to Vancouver on April 21

Pros, cons and tips

Love

  • Easy to get around
  • A huge variety of itineraries
  • Classic Disney ocean-liner style

Keep in mind

  • Fewer big attractions than the newer ships
  • Fewer verandah rooms than the larger ships

First-timer tips

  • If you've always wanted the Panama Canal, the Magic's 14-night crossings are the way to do it with Disney.
  • Book Palo early; it's small and fills up.
