Orlando, Florida

Universal Orlando Resort hotels, explained

Universal Orlando has eleven on-site hotels in three categories. Every hotel guest gets Early Park Admission, and the top-tier hotels add perks like free Universal Express Unlimited.

Signature Collection

The top tier. Three of these hotels include free Universal Express Unlimited, and four have water taxis to the parks.

Signature Collection Portofino Bay An Italian seaside village by the water, modeled on the town of Portofino. Signature Collection Hard Rock Hotel A music-themed hotel filled with rock memorabilia, closest of the Signature hotels to the main parks. Signature Collection Royal Pacific A South Pacific island escape with lush, tropical grounds. Signature Collection Sapphire Falls A Caribbean island resort built around waterfalls and a lagoon. Signature Collection Helios Grand A celestial-themed grand hotel inside Epic Universe, with its own entrance into the park.

Prime Value

Mid-priced hotels with modern style. Stella Nova has a walking path to Epic Universe.

Prime Value Aventura A sleek, modern high-rise with a rooftop bar. Prime Value Stella Nova A space-age hotel inspired by the stars and constellations, next to Epic Universe. Opened in 2025. Prime Value Terra Luna Stella Nova's twin, with a bright, otherworldly look near Epic Universe.

Value Inns & Suites

The lowest-priced on-site hotels, with family suites at several of them.

Value Inns & Suites Cabana Bay A retro 1950s and 1960s beach-motel look, with family suites and plenty to do. Value Inns & Suites Surfside A laid-back surf and beach theme at one of Universal's lowest prices. Value Inns & Suites Dockside Surfside's sister hotel, with the same beachy theme and value pricing.

What every hotel guest gets

  • Early Park Admission up to an hour before opening at select attractions. Valid park admission is required.
  • Free transportation by shuttle bus or water taxi from select hotels, plus walking paths.
  • Charge purchases to your room key across the parks, hotels and CityWalk.
  • Free delivery of purchases from select park stores to your hotel (not on check-out day).

Universal Express Unlimited is included only at Loews Portofino Bay, Hard Rock Hotel and Loews Royal Pacific. It works at select rides at Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure, not at Volcano Bay or Epic Universe.

Prices, perks and transportation change. We checked Universal's and Loews' benefit pages in November 2026; confirm details with Universal before you book.
