BoardWalk Villas Resort: The Full Guide

BoardWalk Villas share the BoardWalk Inn building and the same walk and Friendship boats to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. Deluxe Studios sleep five; one-bedroom villas sleep four; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. Luna Park Pool is scheduled to close January 11 through April 2027; the Villas Pool and the Inn Pool are planned to stay open.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's BoardWalk, in the EPCOT Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
2101 Epcot Resorts Boulevard, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same building campus as BoardWalk Inn; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
Villas in the same BoardWalk complex as the Inn, facing the promenade or looking at gardens, the pool or the grounds.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 5; one-bedroom villas sleep 4; two-bedroom villas sleep 9 (listed on Disney's 2026 vacation-points chart); three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum. The 2026 cash-rate table we used did not list a two-bedroom cash category.
Beds
Studio: 1 queen, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king and 1 queen-size pull-down. Two-bedroom: typically a lock-off of a one-bedroom and a studio [verify current bed layout]. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 queen-size sleeper sofa.
Getting around
Walking paths and Friendship boats to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. Buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
Shared with BoardWalk Inn and the BoardWalk restaurants. See the Inn guide for the list we last checked.
Pools
Luna Park Pool (200-foot slide) plus two leisure pools. Official pages say Luna Park, the Crazy Play Area and Leaping Horse Libations close January 11 through April 2027; the Villas Pool and the Inn Pool are planned to stay open.

Who BoardWalk Villas are best for

  • Families who want a kitchenette and a fifth bed in a studio, on the same walk or Friendship boat as the Inn, to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios.
  • People who want a Boardwalk View and will pay more for it than for a Resort or Garden or Pool View.
  • Groups who need a two-bedroom villa that sleeps nine, or a Grand Villa that sleeps twelve, in the same building as the promenade restaurants.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a one-bedroom. Official listings cap the one-bedroom at four adults.
  • You are traveling between mid-January and April 2027 and were counting on Luna Park. Official pages say that pool, the play area and Leaping Horse Libations will be closed then.
  • You are booking cash and assumed a two-bedroom would be listed at a rack rate. Disney's 2026 vacation-points chart lists two-bedroom villas that sleep nine, usually as a lock-off of a studio and a one-bedroom. The 2026 cash-rate table we used did not list that category. Room types, sizes and beds

This guide covers BoardWalk Villas. The hotel rooms at BoardWalk Inn have their own page. Guests share the promenade, the Friendship boats, the walk to EPCOT and the pools. There is no extra-adult charge up to the occupancy limit.

Pools and things to do

  • Luna Park Pool is the main, carnival-themed pool, 184,217 gallons, with a 200-foot Keister Coaster slide, a kiddie pool and a whirlpool spa. Official pages say this pool, the Luna Park Crazy Play Area and Leaping Horse Libations will close January 11, 2027, through April 2027 for routine maintenance. Guests may see or hear work during the day.
  • Two leisure pools are planned to stay open during that closure. Official BoardWalk Villas pool pages place one in the Rose Garden Courtyard and one next to the Community Hall building; official notices also call these the Villas Pool and the Inn Pool. Both have whirlpool spas.

Pros

  • Walk or Friendship boat to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios
  • Studios that sleep five, including a Boardwalk View option
  • Grand Villas that sleep twelve, in the same complex as the promenade
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • One-bedroom villas sleep four, not five
  • Luna Park is scheduled to close mid-January through April 2027
  • A standalone two-bedroom was not in the 2026 cash-rate table we used
  • Magic Kingdom and Animal Kingdom are buses

Booking and value tips

  • If you need five people in one unit, book a studio, not a one-bedroom.
  • If you are staying between mid-January and April 2027, plan on the leisure pools rather than Luna Park.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
