Polynesian Villas & Bungalows Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

Read the full guide

  • Category and price

    Deluxe Villa studios in the Polynesian longhouses, plus bungalows over Seven Seas Lagoon. Island Tower has its own page. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio ran about $771 to $1,648 a night at full price, tax included. Bungalows ran about $3,662 to $7,045.

  • Getting to the parks

    Monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center. Boat to Magic Kingdom. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Longhouse Deluxe Studios sleep 5 (a queen, a queen-size pull-down and a single pull-down). Bungalows sleep 8 and have a lake view. Room sizes were not confirmed on a current official page we opened [verify].

  • Best for

    Families who want a monorail to Magic Kingdom and a fifth bed in a studio, or groups who specifically want a bungalow on the lagoon. A weaker pick if you meant to book Island Tower — Tower Deluxe Studios sleep four, not five.

  • Food

    Shared with Polynesian Village, including 'Ohana, Kona Cafe and Capt. Cook's. Wailulu Bar & Grill is in the Island Tower lobby on the same campus, with an official quick-service breakfast from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. and table-service lunch and dinner.

  • Pools

    Shared with the Polynesian, including the Lava Pool. We did not get a clean official villas pool page when we checked [verify current pool list].

Heads up: Island Tower is the newer building on this campus (opened 2024) and is covered on its own page, so longhouse studios and bungalows are not mixed in with Duo Studios and tower villas.
