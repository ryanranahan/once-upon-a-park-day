BoardWalk Inn: The Full Guide

This is a Deluxe hotel on Crescent Lake, with a walk or a Friendship boat to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios, and the BoardWalk right outside the door. Many rooms can sleep five, depending on the bed setup, and the dining includes Flying Fish and Trattoria al Forno.

Hotel rooms are in the Inn buildings along the BoardWalk and around the courtyard. The property is shared with BoardWalk Villas.
Rooms
Standard rooms are often listed around 434 sq ft [verify]. Many sleep up to 5, but the bed setup varies: some rooms have 2 queen beds and a day bed, and others have 2 queens or a king, with or without a day bed. Views are Resort or Water. Club Level rooms include lounge access.
Beds
It depends on the category. Check the bed notes in the room table before you book if you need a day bed or a specific layout.
Getting around
Walking paths and Friendship boats to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios; buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
BoardWalk Deli, Trattoria al Forno, Flying Fish, plus other restaurants on the BoardWalk, including The Cake Bake Shop
Pools
Luna Park Pool (the main pool, with a 200-foot slide) and two leisure pools. Luna Park is scheduled to close January 11 through April 2027 for maintenance.

Who BoardWalk Inn is best for

  • Anyone who wants to walk or take a boat to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios. The International Gateway and the Friendship boat dock are close, and Hollywood Studios is a walk or a boat ride too.
  • People who like having restaurants and evening activity right outside the hotel. The BoardWalk is the front yard here, with table-service dining, a deli, and more a short stroll away.
  • Families who can confirm the bed setup they need. Many standard rooms can sleep five, but not every room includes a day bed.

Think twice if…

  • You want a quiet hotel with little going on after dinner. The BoardWalk stays busy in the evening, and rooms that face it can pick up some of that energy.
  • You are visiting between mid-January and April 2027 and the Luna Park slide is the reason you booked here. Official pages say that pool, the play area and Leaping Horse Libations will close for maintenance. The two leisure pools are planned to stay open.
  • You need a guaranteed five-person layout without checking the room notes. The 2026 rate sheet lists more than one bed setup in the same category.

Getting to the parks

This is one of the easier resorts for EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. You can walk, or you can take a Friendship boat across Crescent Lake. Official transportation listings also support those water routes. Buses cover the parks the walk and the boats don't reach.

Dining

  • BoardWalk Deli is the quick-service restaurant at the Inn, listed at 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked, with sandwiches and other deli food for breakfast through dinner. [verify hours still current]
  • Trattoria al Forno serves Italian breakfast and dinner on the BoardWalk. Disney listed breakfast 7:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked. Official pages say character dining is currently not available here. [verify hours still current]
  • Flying Fish is the Signature dinner restaurant, listed at 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. when we checked, for seafood and oak-grilled steaks. There is a dress code, and advance reservations are important. [verify hours still current]
  • The Cake Bake Shop is another table-service restaurant on the BoardWalk, a short walk from the Inn.
  • Leaping Horse Libations is the pool bar at Luna Park. Official pages say it will close with the main pool from January 11 through April 2027.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Flying Fish books up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • Luna Park Pool is the main, carnival-themed pool, at 184,217 gallons, with a 200-foot slide, a kiddie pool and a whirlpool spa. Official pages say this pool, the Luna Park Crazy Play Area and Leaping Horse Libations will close January 11, 2027, through April 2027 for routine maintenance. Guests may see or hear work during the day.
  • Two leisure pools sit on the property, one south of Luna Park and one on the east side of the hotel. Official pages also call these the Villas Pool and the Inn Pool, and they are planned to stay open during the Luna Park closure. Both have whirlpool spas.
  • Towels are free at all of the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

  • A distinctive main pool, plus two quieter leisure pools when you want less activity

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Resort View room if the BoardWalk or lake view is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran from $686 to $1,185 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest hotel rate at this resort.
  • Pay more for Water View only if you will use it. Those rooms ran about $820 to $1,267, and they look at the BoardWalk or Crescent Lake. If you are a light sleeper, a garden or courtyard view may be the better night.
  • If you need five or six people in one room, read the bed notes before you book, or look at a Deluxe Club Level room, which is larger and includes a sleeper sofa.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • If you are staying between mid-January and April 2027, plan on the leisure pools rather than Luna Park.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
