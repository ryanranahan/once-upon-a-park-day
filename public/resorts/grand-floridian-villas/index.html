Grand Floridian Villas Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of The Villas at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

Read the full guide

  • Category and price

    Deluxe Villa resort at the Grand Floridian, in the Magic Kingdom area. In 2026 a Deluxe or Resort Studio ran about $832 to $1,793 a night at full price, tax included, depending on the view. One-bedroom villas ran about $1,182 to $2,257, two-bedroom villas about $1,882 to $3,599, and Grand Villas about $3,664 to $6,518.

  • Getting to the parks

    Monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center. Boat to Magic Kingdom, plus a walking path. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Deluxe Studios, Resort Studios and one-bedroom villas sleep 5. Two-bedroom villas sleep 9. Grand Villas sleep 12. A Resort Studio has two queens and a day bed; a Deluxe Studio has pull-down beds. Room sizes were not confirmed on a current official page we opened [verify].

  • Best for

    Families who want a monorail to Magic Kingdom and a kitchen, including a fifth bed in a studio. A weaker pick if the nightly rate is the deciding factor, or if you wanted a standalone villa resort with its own restaurant.

  • Food

    Shared with the Grand Floridian, including Citricos, Narcoossee's, Grand Floridian Cafe and Gasparilla Island Grill. There is no separate villas restaurant.

  • Pools

    Beach Pool, 111,261 gallons, with a 181-foot slide and zero-depth entry, overlooking Seven Seas Lagoon. Courtyard Pool is the quieter leisure pool behind the main building.

Heads up: No official closure unique to the villas turned up when we checked in November 2026. Confirm any hotel-wide work on Disney's official pages before you travel. [verify current notices]
