Contemporary: The Full Guide

This is a Deluxe hotel next to Magic Kingdom, with a monorail that runs through the building and a walking path to the park. Most standard rooms sleep five, and the dining includes Chef Mickey's and California Grill on the 15th floor.

Who the Contemporary is best for

  • Anyone who wants the shortest trip to Magic Kingdom. You can walk, or you can ride the monorail that stops inside the hotel.
  • Families who need a standard room that sleeps five. Most rooms here include a day bed, which is more capacity than a typical Deluxe room at Wilderness Lodge or Animal Kingdom Lodge.
  • People who want character dining and a fireworks-view restaurant without leaving the hotel. Chef Mickey's is on the concourse, and California Grill is on the 15th floor.

Think twice if…

  • You want a quieter hotel. This is one of the busiest Deluxe resorts, and the atrium can feel lively from morning to night.
  • You are staying in 2026 or 2027 and want to avoid construction. Refurbishment work in the atrium, on the Main Tower exterior and at Bay Lake Tower is expected to continue through late 2027. Views and some outdoor paths may be affected. [verify current status]
  • A Theme Park View room is the only reason you booked here, and the budget is tight. Those rooms cost more than Garden Wing rooms, and the view is of Magic Kingdom and the parking area.

Getting to the parks

This is one of the easiest resorts for Magic Kingdom. The monorail station is inside the hotel, and there is also a walking path to the park. Official water transportation listings show boats that connect this resort with Wilderness Lodge and Fort Wilderness. Buses cover the parks the monorail and boats don't reach.

Dining

  • Chef Mickey's is the character buffet, with Mickey Mouse and some of his friends moving through the dining rooms. Disney listed breakfast 7:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. when we checked. Advance reservations are important. [verify hours still current]
  • California Grill is the Signature dinner restaurant on the 15th floor, with views of Seven Seas Lagoon and Magic Kingdom. Disney listed dinner 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked, with a three-course menu. The restaurant pipes in the fireworks music, and fireworks can be cancelled in bad weather. There is a dress code, and you check in on the second floor before a reserved elevator takes you up. [verify hours and menu price still current]
  • Steakhouse 71 serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. Disney listed breakfast 7:30 a.m. to 10:50 a.m., lunch 11:45 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. when we checked. There is also a lounge. [verify hours still current]
  • Contempo Cafe is the quick-service restaurant on the Grand Canyon Concourse, listed at 6:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked, for breakfast, lunch and dinner. [verify hours still current]
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Chef Mickey's and California Grill book up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • The Feature Pool is the main pool, with a 17-foot waterslide. Official descriptions also mention whirlpool spas and a water-play area. [verify current pool details]
  • Bay Lake Pool is a quieter leisure pool. Cabanas may be available to rent.
  • Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Garden Wing Resort View room if the tower view is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran from $638 to $1,137 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest hotel rate at this resort, and you still have the monorail and the walk to Magic Kingdom.
  • Pay more for a Main Tower room only if you will use the view or the shorter walk to the monorail. Water View rooms ran about $880 to $1,369. Theme Park View rooms ran about $960 to $1,518.
  • If you need a Club Level room, read the view notes carefully. Some Club rooms look at Bay Lake, and the Theme Park View Atrium Club rooms look at Magic Kingdom.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • If you are staying in 2026 or 2027, read the refurbishment note before you book a specific view.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
