BoardWalk Villas Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's BoardWalk Villas, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Deluxe Villa rooms in the BoardWalk complex. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio ran about $686 to $1,287 a night at full price, tax included, depending on the view. One-bedroom villas ran about $900 to $1,568, and Grand Villas about $3,016 to $5,383. The 2026 cash-rate table we used did not list a standalone two-bedroom category [verify current 2-bedroom cash availability].

  • Getting to the parks

    Walking paths and Friendship boats to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. Buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Deluxe Studios sleep 5. One-bedroom villas sleep 4. Grand Villas sleep 12. Room sizes were not confirmed on a current official page we opened [verify].

  • Best for

    Families who want a kitchenette and a fifth bed in a studio on the BoardWalk, or groups who need a Grand Villa. A weaker pick if you need a fifth bed in a one-bedroom, or if you are staying mid-January through April 2027 and were counting on Luna Park Pool.

  • Food

    Shared with BoardWalk Inn and the promenade restaurants. Leaping Horse Libations is scheduled to close with Luna Park Pool from January 11 through April 2027.

  • Pools

    Luna Park Pool, with a 200-foot slide, plus two leisure pools. Official pages say Luna Park, the Crazy Play Area and Leaping Horse Libations close January 11 through April 2027; the Villas Pool and the Inn Pool are planned to stay open.

Heads up: Official pages say the Luna Park Pool, the Luna Park Crazy Play Area and Leaping Horse Libations will close January 11, 2027, through April 2027 for routine maintenance. The Inn Pool and the Villas Pool are planned to stay open, and guests may see or hear work during the day.
