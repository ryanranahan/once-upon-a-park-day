Polynesian Villas & Bungalows Resort: The Full Guide

These are the Disney Vacation Club studios in the Polynesian longhouses, plus the bungalows over Seven Seas Lagoon. Studios sleep five; bungalows sleep eight. The Island Tower next door has its own page. You share the Polynesian's monorail, the boat to Magic Kingdom, and the hotel restaurants.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Polynesian Village Resort, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
1600 Seven Seas Drive, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same campus as the Polynesian; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
Deluxe Studios are in the Polynesian longhouses. The bungalows sit over Seven Seas Lagoon. Island Tower is a separate building with its own guide.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 5; bungalows sleep 8. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studio: 1 queen, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Bungalow: 1 king, 1 queen, 1 queen-size pull-down and 2 single pull-downs. Lake view on the bungalows.
Getting around
Monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center; boat to Magic Kingdom. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
Shared with the Polynesian Village hotel. Island Tower's Wailulu Bar & Grill is also on the same campus; see that guide for hours we confirmed on Disney's official page.
Pools

Who Polynesian Villas & Bungalows are best for

  • Families who want a monorail to Magic Kingdom and a fifth bed in a studio, without booking a hotel room. Official 2026 listings put these longhouse Deluxe Studios at five adults.
  • Groups who specifically want a bungalow on the water. They sleep eight and look at the lagoon. They are priced in a different range than a studio.
  • People who already like the Polynesian and want a kitchenette or a full bungalow kitchen on the same monorail loop. [verify bungalow kitchen layout]

Think twice if…

  • You meant to book Island Tower. Duo Studios, Tower Deluxe Studios, tower villas and penthouses are on the Island Tower page. A Tower Deluxe Studio sleeps four, not five.
  • The bungalow rate is a shock after looking at studios. In 2026 a bungalow ran about $3,662 to $7,045 a night at full price, tax included.
  • You want a standalone villa resort with no hotel next door. This is still the Polynesian campus, with the same crowds at the monorail and the main pool.

Room types, sizes and beds

This guide covers the longhouse Deluxe Studios and the bungalows at Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows. Island Tower and the hotel rooms at Polynesian Village have their own pages. Guests share the monorail, the boat dock and the hotel restaurants. Studios typically have a kitchenette. There is no extra-adult charge up to the occupancy limit.

Dining

Longhouse and bungalow guests use the Polynesian Village restaurants. See the Polynesian Village full guide for the current list, including 'Ohana, Kona Cafe and Capt. Cook's. Hours change; check Disney's official pages before you book. [verify hours still current]

On the same campus, Wailulu Bar & Grill is in the Island Tower lobby. Disney's official page lists a quick-service breakfast from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., and table-service lunch and dinner from an all-day menu, with indoor and outdoor seating. Check-in is at the podium in the Island Tower lobby. Official text says parking for dining reservations is at the Polynesian Village hotel, not at Island Tower. [verify lunch and dinner hours]

Pros

  • Monorail and boat to Magic Kingdom
  • Longhouse studios that sleep five
  • Bungalows on the lagoon for groups of eight, if the rate works
  • Shared access to the Polynesian restaurants, plus Wailulu at Island Tower

Cons

  • Bungalows are among the highest cash rates on property
  • Easy to confuse these studios with Island Tower categories, which sleep fewer in a Deluxe Studio
  • EPCOT is a monorail transfer; the other parks are buses

Booking and value tips

  • Check the word "Tower" on the room name. If it says Tower, you are in Island Tower, not a longhouse studio.
  • A bungalow is a different product from a two-bedroom villa at another resort. Compare occupancy, the lagoon setting and the rate before you assume it is the better deal.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.

  • Island Tower is the newer building on this campus (opened 2024). It is covered on its own page so the longhouse studios and bungalows are not mixed in with Duo Studios and tower villas.
