Polynesian Village Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Polynesian Village Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

Read the full guide

  • Category and price

    Deluxe resort in the Magic Kingdom area, on the Seven Seas Lagoon. In 2026 a Resort View room ran about $771 to $1,371 a night at full price, tax included. Water View rooms ran about $876 to $1,446, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    Resort monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT with a change at the Transportation and Ticket Center. A walking path also leads to the TTC. A boat (the Gold Launch) runs to Magic Kingdom and also serves the Grand Floridian. Buses go to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Standard hotel rooms are about 415 sq ft [verify] and sleep up to 5 adults. Most are set up with 2 queen beds and a day bed. Some Resort View rooms have 1 king bed and a day bed, or 2 queen beds. Honeymoon Club Level rooms sleep 2, with 1 king bed.

  • Best for

    Families who want monorail or boat access to Magic Kingdom, and anyone who cares about on-site dining. Theme Park View rooms look across the lagoon toward the park. It is a Deluxe price, so it is not the pick if you are mainly watching the nightly rate.

  • Food

    This is a dining resort. 'Ohana serves a character breakfast with Lilo and Stitch, plus a family-style dinner without characters. Kona Cafe is table service for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Capt. Cook's and Chip 'n' Dale's Deli are the quick-service spots, and Pineapple Lanai is the walk-up window for pineapple soft-serve. Trader Sam's Grog Grotto is the themed bar.

  • Pools

    The Lava Pool is the main pool, with a volcano, a waterfall, a 142-foot slide and zero-depth entry. The Oasis Pool is a quieter garden pool. Guests can also use the Cove Pool and Moana's Voyage splash area at Island Tower.

Heads up: Disney has listed construction on the front drive and bus area from May 2025 through 2026, and exterior maintenance starting Jan 15, 2026, that may block lagoon views from 'Ohana. Restaurants, pools and amenities were planned to stay open. [verify if this work is still underway]
