Island Tower Resort: The Full Guide

Island Tower is the newer Disney Vacation Club tower at the Polynesian, on Seven Seas Lagoon. Duo Studios sleep two; Tower Deluxe Studios sleep four; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; penthouses sleep eight. Wailulu Bar & Grill is in the tower lobby. You use the Polynesian monorail and the boat to Magic Kingdom.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
1600 Seven Seas Drive, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same campus as the Polynesian; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
A high-rise tower on the lagoon, opened in 2024, next to the Polynesian Village hotel and the older villas and bungalows.
Rooms
Duo Studios sleep 2; Tower Deluxe Studios sleep 4; one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas sleep 9; two-bedroom penthouses sleep 8. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Duo Studio: 1 queen-size pull-down. Tower Deluxe Studio: 1 queen and 1 queen-size pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Two-bedroom villa: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Penthouse: 1 king, 2 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down.
Getting around
Monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center, via the Polynesian; boat to Magic Kingdom. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs. Official dining text also mentions a walk from the Transportation and Ticket Center to the tower.
Food
Wailulu Bar & Grill in the Island Tower lobby, plus the Polynesian Village restaurants on the same campus
Pools
Guests can use the Polynesian pools, including the Lava Pool. Whether the tower has its own pool is [verify].

Who Island Tower is best for

  • Two adults who want a small, newer room on the monorail. The Duo Studio is listed as one queen-size pull-down, sleeping two, at a lower rack range than a Tower Deluxe Studio.
  • Families who want a Theme Park View from the tower and will pay for it. Official listings include Magic Kingdom and Seven Seas Lagoon views on several categories.
  • People who want table-service dining in the same building. Wailulu Bar & Grill is in the tower lobby, with indoor and outdoor seating.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio. A Tower Deluxe Studio sleeps four. The longhouse Deluxe Studios on the Polynesian Villas page are the ones listed as sleeping five.
  • You are booking a dining reservation and plan to park at the tower. Official text says parking for Wailulu reservations is at the Polynesian Village hotel, not at Island Tower. Walk-up waitlist guests are told to park at the Transportation and Ticket Center and monorail or walk from there.
  • You assumed every table at Wailulu includes fireworks. Official text says Magic Kingdom nighttime entertainment is not guaranteed, some tables may not have a fireworks view, and specific seating requests are not accommodated.

Dining

  • Wailulu Bar & Grill is in the Island Tower lobby. Disney's official page lists a quick-service, grab-and-go breakfast from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m., and table-service lunch and dinner from an all-day menu, with indoor and outdoor seating. Check in at the podium in the lobby. Official text says parking for reservations is at Disney's Polynesian Village Resort, not at Island Tower, and that fireworks viewing is not guaranteed. Pros

  • Newer tower on the monorail loop, with Wailulu in the lobby
  • Duo Studios for two people, at a lower rack range than a Deluxe Studio
  • Theme Park Views of Magic Kingdom and Seven Seas Lagoon on several categories
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Tower Deluxe Studios sleep four, not five
  • Dining parking is not at the tower, per official text
  • Fireworks are not guaranteed with a Wailulu reservation
  • EPCOT is a monorail transfer; the other parks are buses

Booking and value tips

  • If you need five people in a studio, look at a longhouse Deluxe Studio, not a Tower Deluxe Studio.
  • The 2026 cash-rate table we used listed a two-bedroom villa only in Theme Park View. If you need another view, confirm current availability with Disney before you assume it is for sale at cash rates.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
