Kidani Village Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Animal Kingdom Villas – Kidani Village, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Deluxe Villa village next to Animal Kingdom Lodge. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio ran about $516 to $1,227 a night at full price, tax included, depending on the view. One-bedroom villas ran about $853 to $1,783, two-bedroom villas about $1,284 to $3,121, and Grand Villas about $2,751 to $4,989.

  • Getting to the parks

    Buses to all four parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. There is no monorail, boat or Skyliner from this resort.

  • Room size

    Deluxe Studios sleep 4. One-bedroom villas sleep 5. Two-bedroom villas sleep 9. Grand Villas sleep 12. Savanna View looks at one of the animal savannas; Resort View can mean the pool or parking. Room sizes were not confirmed on a current official page we opened [verify].

  • Best for

    Families who want a savanna view and a kitchen, and who will use Sanaa and Samawati Springs Pool. A weaker pick if you need a fifth bed in a studio, or a short ride to Magic Kingdom or EPCOT.

  • Food

    Sanaa, in Kidani Village, with a quick-service breakfast and table-service lunch and dinner. Guests typically can also use the restaurants at Jambo House [verify shared dining access].

  • Pools

    Samawati Springs Pool at Kidani, with a 128-foot slide, zero-depth entry and the Uwanja Camp water playground. Official pages also describe Uzima Springs Pool at Jambo House.

Heads up: No unique official closure for Kidani Village turned up when we checked in November 2026. Confirm current notices on Disney's official pages before you travel. [verify current notices]
