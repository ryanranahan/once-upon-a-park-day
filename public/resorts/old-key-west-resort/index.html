Old Key West Resort: The Full Guide

Old Key West is a spread-out Disney Vacation Club resort near Disney Springs, with a boat down the Sassagoula River. Deluxe Studios sleep four, with two queen beds; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. Olivia's Cafe is the table-service restaurant, and Sandcastle Pool is the main pool.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort in the Disney Springs area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
Confirm the street address and check-in building on your reservation. The main building is Hospitality House. [verify current address]
Size
A large, spread-out resort of Key West-style villa buildings along waterways, woodlands and fairways. Budget time to get from a distant building to the boat and to dining.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 4; one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas sleep 9; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studio: 2 queen beds. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size sleeper sofa and 1 twin-size sleeper chair. Two-bedroom: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size sleeper sofa and 1 twin-size sleeper chair. Grand Villa: 1 king, 2 queens, 2 doubles, 1 queen-size sleeper sofa and 1 twin-size sleeper chair.
Getting around
Sassagoula River Cruise boat to Disney Springs (this is not a Friendship boat to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios). Buses to all four parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. An internal bus around the resort is often described by guests; confirm it on arrival. [verify internal bus]
Food
Olivia's Cafe, Good's Food To Go and Turtle Shack Poolside Snacks, all listed on Disney's official Old Key West dining page
Pools
Sandcastle Pool (125-foot slide, 149,600 gallons, near Hospitality House) plus 3 leisure pools at Old Turtle Pond, South Point and Miller's Road

Who Old Key West is best for

  • Families who want more space than a Moderate hotel room, at a lower Deluxe Villa rack range than the monorail villas. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio ran about $500 to $845 a night at full price, tax included.
  • Groups using a two-bedroom or a Grand Villa, especially if a boat to Disney Springs matters more than a walk to a theme park.
  • People who don't mind a spread-out resort and will plan the walk or internal ride from a far building.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio. Official 2026 listings cap the Deluxe Studio at four adults, with two queen beds and no pull-down listed.
  • You thought the boat went to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios. Official water-transportation listings put Old Key West on the Sassagoula River Cruise to Disney Springs, not on the Friendship line.
  • A short walk to a park is the main reason you're booking a Deluxe. All four parks are buses from here.

Pools and things to do

  • Sandcastle Pool is the feature pool near Hospitality House: a 125-foot slide through a sandcastle, 149,600 gallons, a sand beach, a kiddie pool, a whirlpool spa and a dry sauna in the pool-deck lighthouse, per Disney's official pool page.
  • Three leisure pools, each with a whirlpool spa, are in the Old Turtle Pond, South Point and Miller's Road sections.

Pros

  • Roomy villas at a lower 2026 rack range than the monorail DVC resorts
  • Boat to Disney Springs
  • Four pools, including a slide pool at Hospitality House
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Studios sleep four, with two queens and no listed pull-down
  • All four theme parks are buses
  • The layout is spread out; a far building adds walking or waiting time
  • The boat does not go to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios

Booking and value tips

  • Ask how far the building is from Hospitality House, the boat and Sandcastle Pool if a long walk would bother you.
  • If you need a fifth person in one studio, this is not the category for it. Look at a one-bedroom here, or a studio at a resort that lists a pull-down and a fifth adult.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
