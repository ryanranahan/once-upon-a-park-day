Disney's All-Star Movies Resort

Bright, playful buildings decorated with giant icons from classic Disney films.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Last updated November 2026

Overview

All-Star Movies is one of Disney's three original Value resorts. Buildings are grouped by film, with giant movie icons towering over the courtyards, so it's colorful and fun for kids without a deluxe price tag.

First-hand notes coming soon.

The photos here are our own, but the text is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Families on a budget who plan to spend most of each day in the parks and just need a clean, cheerful place to sleep.

Rooms

Standard rooms are compact and sleep up to four, with two queen beds or one queen plus a pull-down bed in refreshed rooms. Check the latest room setup when you book.

Preferred rooms sit closer to the main building and food court, which saves a lot of walking.

Pools and things to do

The main pool is the Fantasia Pool, with a smaller Duck Pond Pool nearby. There's also a kids' splash area beside Fantasia Pool, a playground and the Reel Fun Arcade. Both pools have the same playful, oversized theming as the rest of the resort.

Where to eat

Dining is the World Premiere Food Court, with grab-and-go items and Silver Screen Spirits, a bar by the pool. There's also The Reel Spot, a covered patio with tables. There's no table-service restaurant on site.

Eating with food allergies

Disney is great with food allergies, and the World Premiere Food Court makes it easy. There's a sign that says "Allergy Requests Order Here" at the Lyric counter, so you know exactly where to go.

My wife Jackie ordered there and spoke with the head chef, who went through the order with her and wrote everything down to make sure it was safe. She got a gluten-free cheese pizza, which came in its own box with a purple "Requested to be made without" sticker, with gluten and wheat checked and "Cheese Pizza" written on the lid, so we could see at a glance it had been made for her.

How ordering works at the food court

  • Tell the cast member when you order. Mention every allergy in your party. If you can't find something that works, ask to speak with a special diets-trained cast member.
  • Use the allergy-friendly menu. Disney's allergy-friendly menus cover the nine most common allergens: eggs, fish, milk, peanuts, sesame, shellfish, soy, tree nuts and wheat. Kitchens will also do their best with requests like gluten, corn or citrus.
  • Mobile ordering works too. In the My Disney Experience app, tap the Allergy-friendly icon on the menu screen. When you pick up, you'll speak to a cast member so you can confirm the order.
  • Look for the marker. Allergy orders come with a pick in the food, a designated tray or, for to-go meals, a sticker on the box and bag. If you don't see one, ask before you eat.

Before your trip

  • If your party has four or more allergy requests, or someone has a medically restrictive diet, email Disney's special diets team at Special.Diets@DisneyWorld.com, no more than 14 days before you arrive.
  • Disney takes steps to avoid cross-contact but can't guarantee any item is completely allergen-free, and kitchens aren't separate. For severe allergies, always talk it through with a cast member.
  • As of October 6, 2026, Disney follows the FDA's updated list and no longer treats coconut and a few other nuts as tree nuts. If you're allergic to coconut, say so specifically.

Getting to the parks

  • Buses to all four parks and Disney Springs

Every park is reached by Disney bus. Plan extra time at busy times of day.

Pros and cons

Love

  • One of the lowest-priced Disney-owned hotels
  • Bright, kid-friendly theming
  • You still get Disney resort perks such as Early Theme Park Entry

Keep in mind

  • Buses are the only Disney transportation
  • No table-service restaurant
  • Rooms are simple and on the small side

First-timer tips

  • Request a Preferred room or a building near the main building if you don't want a long walk.
  • Mobile order breakfast from the food court before you head to the bus.
  • Take a look at the other two All-Star resorts next door; they're within walking distance.
