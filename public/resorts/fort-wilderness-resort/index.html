Fort Wilderness Campground Resort: The Full Guide

Fort Wilderness Campground is the tent and RV campground on Bay Lake, not a hotel. Sites are listed as sleeping eight, from a tent or pop-up pad up to larger Premium and Premium Meadow sites. You take an internal bus around the property, then a boat to Magic Kingdom. Trail's End Restaurant and Meadow Snack Bar are on site. The cabins have their own page.

Campsites, not hotel rooms. Official 2026 listings put every category at up to 8 adults. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum. There is no extra-adult charge up to that limit.

Who Fort Wilderness Campground is best for

  • People who already camp or travel with an RV and want a boat to Magic Kingdom, with hook-ups and internal buses on site.
  • Families who want the lowest full-price rate on property that still includes official Disney resort transportation. In 2026 a tent or pop-up site ran about $82 to $222 a night at full price, tax included.
  • Groups of up to eight who can live on a campsite and will use the trails, the marina and the Settlement.

Think twice if…

  • You want a hotel room. There is no standard guest room on this page. The cabins are the furnished indoor option, and they have their own guide.
  • You dislike internal buses. Most trips, including the walk-up to the Magic Kingdom boat, start with a loop bus.
  • Your RV or tent will not fit the pad you booked. Official 2026 notes give concrete pad sizes by category. Read them before you assume a Premium Meadow site is just a better Preferred site.

Dining

  • Trail's End Restaurant is the table-service restaurant at the campsites, per Disney's official page. That page listed operating hours of 7:30 a.m. to 9:45 p.m. when we checked, with breakfast, lunch and dinner menus, including a fried-chicken dinner. [verify hours still current]
  • Meadow Snack Bar is an official quick-service kiosk, with salads, sandwiches, light snacks, frozen treats, beer and sodas. Hours were not listed clearly on the page we opened. [verify hours]

Booking and value tips

  • Match the pad size to your equipment before you fall for a lower rate. Tent and pop-up pads are listed at 10 by 25 feet. Larger RVs need the 18-by-60 Premium or Premium Meadow pads in the 2026 notes.
  • Preferred is about being closer to the marina, not a larger pad than Full Hook-Up. Premium and Premium Meadow are the larger pads.
  • If you want walls, a kitchen and beds already in the unit, look at the cabins instead of stretching a tent booking.
