Port Orleans French Quarter Resort: The Full Guide

This is the more compact side of Port Orleans, with a food court, a lively main pool, and a boat down the Sassagoula River to Disney Springs. Theme parks are a bus ride.

Who Port Orleans French Quarter is best for

  • People who want a smaller Moderate. This side of Port Orleans is more compact than Riverside, and you are close to the food court, the pool and the boat dock if you book a Preferred room.
  • Anyone who wants a boat to Disney Springs. Official water transportation listings show the Sassagoula River Cruise from French Quarter, Riverside, Old Key West and Saratoga Springs.
  • Families of four who want two queen beds at a Moderate rate. In 2026 a Standard Location room ran about $322 to $483 a night at full price, tax included.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed for a child, or a more themed "Royal" room. Those are on the Riverside side, not here.
  • You want several table-service restaurants on site. The food court is the main option while Scat Cat's Club is under refurbishment. Riverside's Boatwright's Dining Hall is a walk or a short boat ride away.
  • Your parks are the main event and you want a Skyliner or monorail. Theme parks are buses from here.

Room types, sizes and beds

Standard and Preferred rooms are the same layout. You are paying for location. Official listings describe views of the garden, courtyard, riverfront or parking area. Preferred rooms are listed as close to the lobby, dining, shopping and transportation.

Dining

  • Sassagoula Floatworks and Food Factory is the quick-service food court, and the main place for breakfast, lunch and dinner while the lounge and café are being refurbished.
  • Scat Cat's Club – Café closed for refurbishment beginning October 6, 2026, and is expected to reopen in December 2026. Scat Cat's Club – Lounge is expected to reopen in January 2027. Confirm both are open before you plan on them.
  • Port Orleans Riverside, next door, has Boatwright's Dining Hall for table service.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

Pools and things to do

  • Doubloon Lagoon is the main pool. Official pages describe a 51-foot sea-serpent slide (Scales), a water play area for guests 48 inches or under, a whirlpool spa and the Mardi Grogs pool bar.
  • Official pool pages also say French Quarter guests can use Ol' Man Island at Port Orleans Riverside, a short walk or boat ride down the river, with a 95-foot slide, a whirlpool spa and a kiddie pool.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

  • Access to Riverside's Ol' Man Island pool as well as Doubloon Lagoon

Booking and value tips

  • Book a Standard Location room if you don't mind a short walk. In 2026 those rooms ran about $322 to $483 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. Preferred Location rooms ran about $356 to $557. King rooms ran about $375 to $578.
  • Pay for Preferred if you want to be closer to the lobby, the food court and the boat dock. The room is the same layout.
  • Need a child-size pull-down bed or a Royal Guest Room? Those categories are at Riverside, not here.
  • Extra adults cost more. Moderate rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $25 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
