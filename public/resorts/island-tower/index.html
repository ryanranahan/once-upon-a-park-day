Deluxe Villa resort · Walt Disney World · Magic Kingdom area

Island Tower at Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows

The newest Disney Vacation Club tower at the Polynesian, on the Seven Seas Lagoon.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Facts last checked November 2026

This is a Disney Vacation Club resort.

You don't need to be a member to stay here. Villas range from studios with a kitchenette to multi-bedroom villas with a full kitchen. It shares grounds with Polynesian Village.

Overview

Island Tower is the newest Disney Vacation Club building at the Polynesian, a tower on the Seven Seas Lagoon with views toward Magic Kingdom.

First-hand notes coming soon.

This guide is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take, photos and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Families who want a modern villa with the Polynesian's location and amenities.

Rooms

As a Disney Vacation Club resort, rooms range from studios with a kitchenette to multi-bedroom villas with a full kitchen and washer and dryer. You don't need to be a member to stay; you can book with cash like a regular hotel.

Pools and things to do

Island Tower has its own pool area, and guests can use the Polynesian's pools too.

Where to eat

Guests use the Polynesian's restaurants, including 'Ohana, Kona Cafe and Pineapple Lanai.

Getting to the parks

  • Monorail to Magic Kingdom and Epcot (Epcot via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center) via the Polynesian
  • Boat to Magic Kingdom
  • Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, and Disney Springs

Pros and cons

Love

  • Newest villas at the Polynesian
  • Lagoon and Magic Kingdom views
  • Monorail and boat access

Keep in mind

  • High price
  • A walk to the Polynesian's monorail station
  • Busy shared areas

First-timer tips

  • Request a Magic Kingdom-facing room for fireworks views.
  • Use the boat to Magic Kingdom for a relaxing ride.

Quick answers

What kind of hotel is Island Tower?
Island Tower at Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows is a Deluxe Villa resort in the Magic Kingdom area of Walt Disney World. The newest Disney Vacation Club tower at the Polynesian, on the Seven Seas Lagoon. It's a Disney Vacation Club resort, but you don't need to be a member to stay here.
Who is Island Tower best for?
Island Tower is best for families who want a modern villa at the Polynesian.
How do you get from Island Tower to the parks?
From Island Tower, your options are monorail to Magic Kingdom and Epcot (Epcot via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center) via the Polynesian; boat to Magic Kingdom; or buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, and Disney Springs.
What are the downsides of staying at Island Tower?
Things to keep in mind: High price. A walk to the Polynesian's monorail station. Busy shared areas.
What should first-timers know about Island Tower?
Request a Magic Kingdom-facing room for fireworks views. Use the boat to Magic Kingdom for a relaxing ride.
