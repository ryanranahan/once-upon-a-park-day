Disney's All-Star Sports Resort

Sports-themed buildings with supersized balls, helmets and courts.

By Ryan · Staying here now, November 2026 · Facts last checked November 2026

Our take

I'm staying at All-Star Sports right now, and so far it's made me feel a little differently about the resort than I expected. The rooms aren't as bad as people sometimes make them sound. The refreshed room is simple and clean, and having the vanity outside the bathroom behind a sliding door makes it easier to get ready when you're sharing.

What I'd warn you about is that the resort overall is a little darker than usual, both inside and out, and you notice it most at night. The giant rackets, balls and surfboards are fun to see lit up, but the resort as a whole feels dimmer than other places on property.

What other guests say

Most recent reviews agree the rooms beat their old reputation. All-Star Sports was the last of the three All-Star resorts to get its room refresh, finished around 2023, and reviewers like the two queen beds (one folds up into a table), the laminate floors that replaced the old carpet, and the sliding door to the bathroom. A lot of the harshest reviews you'll find online, the ones that call it the worst resort on property, were written before that refresh. The rooms are still small, and a few reviewers mention thin walls.

The darker feel comes up much less. Most reviews don't mention it, and one ranking of in-room lighting gives All-Star Sports good marks, so how dark it feels may depend on your building and when you're walking around. Bring a small light or use your phone's flashlight if you'll be heading back from the bus late.

Who it suits

Budget-minded families and sports fans who want the lowest-cost Disney-owned option.

Rooms

Standard rooms are compact and sleep up to four, with two queen beds or one queen plus a pull-down bed in refreshed rooms. Check the latest room setup when you book.

In the refreshed rooms the vanity sits outside the bathroom, so one person can get ready while another uses the tub. The tub and shower combo has a frosted-glass sliding door, a built-in tile niche and wall-mounted shampoo, conditioner and body wash.

Pools and things to do

The Surfboard Bay Pool is the main pool, and the Grand Slam Pool is the second.

For a rainy day or a break from the sun, the Game Point Arcade has ball-rolling lanes, basketball hoops and other games. A sign nearby has a QR code for the resort's recreation schedule.

Where to eat

Dining is the End Zone Food Court plus a pool bar. There's no table-service restaurant.

I got a to-go order of the chicken parm with a breadstick and a side of marinara, and it was surprisingly good, good enough that I ended up ordering it again. My biggest tip here is to always mobile order when you can.

If you eat in, there's seating inside, some of it by windows that look out on a giant chess board, and patio tables with umbrellas outside.

Eating with food allergies

Disney is great with food allergies, and the End Zone Food Court makes it easy. There's a counter with a sign that says "Allergy Requests Order Here", so you know exactly where to go, and you're ordering with a cast member who's ready for allergy requests from the start.

We've ordered allergy-friendly meals here, including the sesame chicken and a chicken Alfredo. They were absolutely delicious, and my wife Jackie loved them. The food court team has been amazing with allergies. Our order came with a purple "Requested to be made without" sticker on the lid, with gluten and wheat checked off, so we could see at a glance it had been made for us.

How ordering works at the food court

  • Order at the allergy counter. Tell the cast member about every allergy in your party. If you can't find something that works, ask to speak with a special diets-trained cast member.
  • Use the allergy-friendly menu. Disney's allergy-friendly menus cover the nine most common allergens: eggs, fish, milk, peanuts, sesame, shellfish, soy, tree nuts and wheat. Kitchens will also do their best with requests like gluten, corn or citrus.
  • Mobile ordering works too. In the My Disney Experience app, tap the Allergy-friendly icon on the menu screen. When you pick up, you'll speak to a cast member so you can confirm the order.
  • Look for the marker. Allergy orders come with a pick in the food, a designated tray or, for to-go meals, a sticker on the box and bag. If you don't see one, ask before you eat.

Before your trip

  • If your party has four or more allergy requests, or someone has a medically restrictive diet, email Disney's special diets team at Special.Diets@DisneyWorld.com, no more than 14 days before you arrive.
  • Disney takes steps to avoid cross-contact but can't guarantee any item is completely allergen-free, and kitchens aren't separate. For severe allergies, always talk it through with a cast member.
  • As of October 6, 2026, Disney follows the FDA's updated list and no longer treats coconut and a few other nuts as tree nuts. If you're allergic to coconut, say so specifically.

Getting to the parks

  • Buses to all four parks and Disney Springs

Every park is reached by Disney bus.

Pros and cons

Love

  • Among the lowest prices on Disney property
  • Refreshed rooms that are nicer than their reputation
  • Surprisingly good food court chicken parm
  • A dedicated allergy ordering counter at the food court
  • Fun, easy-to-navigate sports theming
  • Disney resort perks included

Keep in mind

  • The resort feels darker than most, inside and out, especially at night
  • Bus-only transportation
  • No table-service dining
  • Small rooms, and some reviewers mention thin walls

First-timer tips

  • Ask for a building close to the main building and bus stop.
  • Always mobile order at the End Zone Food Court when you can.
  • With food allergies, order at the food court's allergy counter or use the Allergy-friendly icon when you mobile order.
  • Pack snacks; options are limited to the food court.
  • Check the ESPN Wide World of Sports calendar before you book. Youth tournaments can fill the resort with loud groups.
