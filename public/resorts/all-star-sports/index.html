All-Star Sports Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's All-Star Sports Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Value resort in the Animal Kingdom area. In 2026 a Standard Room ran about $149 to $311 a night at full price, tax included. Preferred rooms ran about $187 to $354, depending on the dates. Tax at the All-Star resorts is 13.5%.

  • Getting to the parks

    Buses to all four theme parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. There is no Skyliner, monorail or boat from this resort.

  • Room size

    Standard and Preferred rooms are the same layout, about 260 sq ft [verify], and sleep up to 4 adults, with 1 queen bed and 1 queen-size table bed. Preferred rooms are closer to Stadium Hall. There are no family suites at this resort.

  • Best for

    Families watching the nightly rate who can live with a food court and a bus. A weaker pick if you need to sleep five or six in one room, or if you want a sit-down restaurant at the hotel.

  • Food

    End Zone Food Court and Grandstand Spirits Pool Bar. No table-service restaurant on site.

  • Pools

    Surfboard Bay Pool is the main pool, officially listed at 242,471 gallons, with a kiddie pool behind Stadium Hall. Grand Slam Pool is the second pool.
