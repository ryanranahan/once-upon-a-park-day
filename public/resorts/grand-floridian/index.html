Grand Floridian Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

Read the full guide

  • Category and price

    Deluxe resort in the Magic Kingdom area, on the Seven Seas Lagoon. In 2026 a Resort View room ran about $832 to $1,370 a night at full price, tax included. Water View rooms ran about $864 to $1,506, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    Resort monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT with a change at the Transportation and Ticket Center. A boat (the Gold Launch) runs to Magic Kingdom and also serves the Polynesian Village. Buses go to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Standard hotel rooms are about 448 sq ft [verify] and sleep up to 5 adults, with 2 queen beds and a day bed, or 1 king bed and a day bed. Most standard view rooms are in the outer buildings. Club Level rooms and suites are a separate set of categories, some of them larger.

  • Best for

    Families who want monorail or boat access to Magic Kingdom and a lot of dining on site, including a character buffet. Theme Park View rooms look toward the park. It is a Deluxe price at the high end of the scale, so it is not the pick if the nightly rate is the main concern.

  • Food

    This is one of the biggest dining resorts on property. 1900 Park Fare is the character buffet for breakfast and dinner. Grand Floridian Cafe, Cítricos, Narcoossee's and Victoria & Albert's are table service. Gasparilla Island Grill is the quick-service spot, and Enchanted Rose and Garden View Lounge are the lounges.

  • Pools

    The Beach Pool is the main pool, at 111,261 gallons, with a 181-foot slide, a waterfall, zero-depth entry and a splash area for little kids. The Courtyard Pool is a quieter, heated zero-entry pool behind the main building, with a whirlpool spa.

Heads up: Renovations continue in select areas, including the porte cochere, through early 2027. Disney says guests may see or hear work during the day or need to take alternate paths.
