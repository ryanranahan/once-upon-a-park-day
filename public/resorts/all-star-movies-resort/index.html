All-Star Movies Resort: The Full Guide

This is a Value resort in the Animal Kingdom area, with giant movie icons outside the buildings and a food court instead of a sit-down restaurant. Buses are the way to every park.

Who All-Star Movies is best for

  • Families watching the nightly rate. This is one of the lowest-priced Disney-owned resorts at Walt Disney World. In 2026 a Standard Room ran about $153 to $311 a night at full price, tax included.
  • Parties of four who can live with a Value room. A queen bed and a queen-size table bed (a Murphy bed that folds down from a table) is the usual setup.
  • Anyone fine with a food court and a bus. That's the whole transportation and dining picture here.

Think twice if…

  • You want a sit-down restaurant at the hotel. There's a food court and a pool bar, and that's it.
  • You want Extended Evening Hours. Value resort guests don't get them.
  • You're staying through January 2027 and you're sensitive to construction. Official pages say guest rooms are under planned refurbishment through January 2027. You may see or hear work, and alternate walkways may be in place. The resort stays open.
  • You don't want to walk. Book a Preferred room, which sits closer to Cinema Hall. Standard rooms can be a longer walk with a stroller or tired kids.

Room types, sizes and beds

There are two categories, and they are the same room. You are paying for location, not a different layout. Official room listings describe views of the courtyard, pool or parking area. Preferred rooms are listed as close to Cinema Hall, dining, shopping and transportation.

What's in the room

The queen-size table bed folds down from a table, which frees floor space during the day if you put it back up. A king room will not sleep four the same way. Rooms are under refurbishment through January 2027, so the finishes you get may depend on when you stay [verify current in-room look].

Getting to the parks

Buses are the only free Disney transportation from All-Star Movies. They run to all four theme parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. There is no Skyliner, monorail or boat from this resort. The bus stop is at Cinema Hall.

Dining

  • World Premiere Food Court is the quick-service restaurant, in Cinema Hall. It is the only place on site for a full breakfast, lunch or dinner.
  • Silver Screen Spirits Pool Bar sits by the Fantasia Pool.
  • There is no table-service restaurant at this resort.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

Pools and things to do

  • Fantasia Pool is the main pool, between the Fantasia building and Cinema Hall. Official pages describe a Sorcerer Mickey fountain, with a smaller kiddie pool and a water play area nearby. Poolside activities are listed here on some days, and movies under the stars are a regular All-Star Movies offer.
  • Duck Pond Pool is the second pool, themed after The Mighty Ducks. Official pages list it at 105,438 gallons and describe it as a hockey-rink design. This is usually the quieter of the two.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Pros and cons

  • One of the lowest nightly rates among Disney-owned resorts
  • The table bed folds up during the day, which helps in 260 sq ft
  • Two pools, including a large main pool next to Cinema Hall
  • A short bus ride to Animal Kingdom, relative to most of the property

Booking and value tips

  • Book a Standard Room if you don't mind walking. In 2026 those rooms ran about $153 to $311 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. Preferred rooms ran about $194 to $359.
  • Pay for Preferred only if the shorter walk matters. The room is the same. You are closer to Cinema Hall, the food court and the buses.
  • Extra adults cost more. Value rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $15 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free. Tax at the All-Star resorts is 13.5%.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • If construction bothers you, wait until after January 2027 or pick All-Star Music or All-Star Sports, which did not have a matching room-refurbishment notice when we checked.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.

Rates change

The rate range above is MouseSavers' October 2026 snapshot of Disney's full prices, not a deal. Always check the live price on Disney's site before you book. Prices at this resort include 13.5% tax, which is higher than the 12.5% charged at most other Disney-owned Walt Disney World hotels.

What's changed recently

  • Through January 2027: Official pages say guest rooms are under planned refurbishment. Guests may see or hear construction and may need to use alternate paths. The resort remains open.
  • The same official notice still mentions a phased refurbishment of Donald's Double Feature, the merchandise shop, "through mid-September." Mid-September 2026 has already passed; confirm whether that shop work is finished [verify whether the shop notice is still current].
