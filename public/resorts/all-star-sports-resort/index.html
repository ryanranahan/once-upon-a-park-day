All-Star Sports Resort: The Full Guide

The original All-Star, with sports-themed buildings and a large main pool. Like the other two All-Stars, it is a Value resort with a food court and buses to the parks.

Who All-Star Sports is best for

  • Families watching the nightly rate. In 2026 a Standard Room ran about $149 to $311 a night at full price, tax included, which is the lowest starting Standard rate of the three All-Stars.
  • Parties of four who can live with a Value room. Official listings show a queen bed and a queen-size table bed.
  • Anyone fine with a food court and a bus. That's the whole transportation and dining picture here.

Think twice if…

  • You need a room for five or six. There are no family suites at All-Star Sports. Look at All-Star Music or Art of Animation instead.
  • You want a sit-down restaurant at the hotel. There's a food court and a pool bar, and that's it.
  • You want Extended Evening Hours. Value resort guests don't get them.
  • You don't want to walk. Book a Preferred room, closer to Stadium Hall. Standard rooms can be a longer walk.

Room types, sizes and beds

There are two categories, and they are the same room. You are paying for location, not a different layout. Official room listings describe views of the courtyard, pool or parking area. Preferred rooms are listed as close to Stadium Hall, dining, shopping and transportation.

Getting to the parks

Buses are the only free Disney transportation from All-Star Sports. They run to all four theme parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. There is no Skyliner, monorail or boat from this resort. The bus stop is at Stadium Hall.

Dining

  • End Zone Food Court is the quick-service restaurant, in Stadium Hall. It is the only place on site for a full breakfast, lunch or dinner.
  • Grandstand Spirits Pool Bar sits by the Surfboard Bay Pool.
  • There is no table-service restaurant at this resort.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

Pools and things to do

  • Surfboard Bay Pool is the main pool. Official pages list it at 242,471 gallons and describe a beach-style pool lined with giant, three-story surfboards. A kiddie pool is behind Stadium Hall, next to this pool.
  • Grand Slam Pool is the second pool, shaped like a baseball diamond. Official pages describe a sporty sundeck and large baseball-bat icons around the deck. Gallonage is not listed on the official pool page we opened.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Booking and value tips

  • Book a Standard Room if you don't mind walking. In 2026 those rooms ran about $149 to $311 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. Preferred rooms ran about $187 to $354.
  • Pay for Preferred only if the shorter walk matters. The room is the same. You are closer to Stadium Hall, the food court and the buses.
  • Need to sleep five or six? This resort does not sell family suites. Compare All-Star Music or Art of Animation before you book two rooms here.
  • Extra adults cost more. Value rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $15 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free. Tax at the All-Star resorts is 13.5%.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.

Rates change

The rate range above is MouseSavers' October 2026 snapshot of Disney's full prices, not a deal. Always check the live price on Disney's site before you book. Prices at this resort include 13.5% tax, which is higher than the 12.5% charged at most other Disney-owned Walt Disney World hotels.
