Kidani Village Resort: The Full Guide

Kidani Village is the Disney Vacation Club building next to Animal Kingdom Lodge, with its own savanna views and its own table-service restaurant, Sanaa. Deluxe Studios sleep four; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. Samawati Springs Pool is at this building. All four parks are buses.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge, in the Animal Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
Next to Animal Kingdom Lodge. The lodge hotel lists 2901 Osceola Parkway, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830; confirm Kidani's check-in address on your reservation. [verify Kidani street address]
Size
A separate village building from Jambo House, with its own lobby, Sanaa and Samawati Springs Pool. Savanna views look at one of the animal savannas; Resort Views look at the pool or parking.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 4; one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas sleep 9; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studio: 1 queen and 1 queen-size pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Two-bedroom: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down.
Getting around
Buses to all four parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. There is no monorail, boat or Skyliner from this resort.
Food
Sanaa, in Kidani Village, with a quick-service breakfast and table-service lunch and dinner. Guests typically can also use the restaurants at Jambo House; confirm access if that matters to you. [verify shared dining access]
Pools
Samawati Springs Pool at Kidani (128-foot slide, zero-depth entry, Uwanja Camp water playground). Official Animal Kingdom Lodge pool pages also describe Uzima Springs Pool at Jambo House.

Who Kidani Village is best for

  • Families who want a savanna view and a kitchen, and will pay more for Savanna View than for a Resort View that looks at the pool or parking.
  • People who want table-service dining in the same building. Sanaa is in Kidani Village, with animal views from some seats.
  • Groups who will use Samawati Springs and Uwanja Camp, especially with younger children who fit the playground's age notes.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio. Official 2026 listings cap Deluxe Studios at four adults.
  • A short ride to Magic Kingdom or EPCOT is the main reason you're booking Deluxe. This resort is buses only.
  • You assumed a Resort View still included animals. Official text describes Resort View studios as looking at the pool or parking, and Resort View one-bedrooms as looking at parking.

Pools and things to do

  • Samawati Springs Pool is at Kidani Village. Official Animal Kingdom Lodge pool pages describe a 128-foot slide, zero-depth entry, 4,700 square feet and 118,138 gallons, plus Uwanja Camp, a water playground with three zones. Official text says children 4 and younger can use Observation Station, with a shallow cistern pool and a bubbling geyser, while adults watch nearby.
  • Uzima Springs Pool is at Jambo House: 11,000 square feet, 234,980 gallons, zero-depth entry and a 67-foot slide, facing Uzima Savanna, per the same official pool page.

Pros

  • Savanna View rooms look at one of the animal savannas
  • Sanaa is in the same building
  • Samawati Springs Pool and Uwanja Camp are at Kidani, with Uzima Springs nearby
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Studios sleep four, not five
  • Resort View can mean parking
  • All parks are buses; there is no monorail, boat or Skyliner
  • Savanna View costs more, and animals are not a guarantee at any given hour

Booking and value tips

  • If the animals are the reason you are booking Kidani, pay for Savanna View and read the Resort View descriptions so you don't end up looking at parking.
  • A studio here sleeps four. If you need five in one unit, look at a one-bedroom.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
