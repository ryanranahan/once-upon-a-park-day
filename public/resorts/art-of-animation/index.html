Disney's Art of Animation Resort

Themed to animated films, with most rooms set up as family suites.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Last updated November 2026

Overview

Art of Animation is a Value resort themed to animated films, and most of its rooms are Family Suites. It shares the Skyliner station with Pop Century.

First-hand notes coming soon.

This guide is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take, photos and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Families of five or six who want a suite with room to spread out, at a lower price than a Moderate or Deluxe resort.

Rooms

Most rooms are Family Suites that sleep up to six, with a separate bedroom, two bathrooms and a kitchenette. There are also standard rooms that sleep four.

Pools and things to do

The Big Blue Pool is the main pool and one of the largest at Walt Disney World, with underwater speakers. There are two smaller pools in other sections.

Where to eat

Dining is the Landscape of Flavors food court, with a pool bar nearby. There's no table-service restaurant.

Getting to the parks

  • Disney Skyliner gondolas to Epcot and Hollywood Studios
  • Buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, and Disney Springs

Pros and cons

Love

  • Family Suites with a kitchenette and two bathrooms
  • Skyliner access to Epcot and Hollywood Studios
  • Immersive theming kids love

Keep in mind

  • Suites cost more than standard Value rooms
  • Buses to Magic Kingdom and Animal Kingdom
  • No table-service dining

First-timer tips

  • Compare a suite with two Value rooms; the suite's extra bathroom is a big plus for families.
  • The Skyliner station is a short walk from the main building.
