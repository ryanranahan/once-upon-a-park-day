Yacht Club Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Yacht Club Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Deluxe resort in the EPCOT area, sharing grounds and Stormalong Bay with Beach Club. In 2026 a Resort View room ran about $648 to $1,138 a night at full price, tax included. Water View rooms ran about $760 to $1,236, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    Walking path and Friendship boats to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. The EPCOT Skyliner station is a walk through the International Gateway. Buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Resort View and Water View rooms sleep up to 5, about 400 sq ft [verify]. Club Level rooms include lounge access. Confirm the bed setup if you need a day bed. Suites, including turret rooms, are also offered.

  • Best for

    Anyone who wants to walk or boat to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios, with Yachtsman Steakhouse and Ale & Compass in the hotel. A weaker pick if Magic Kingdom is your main park, or if you want Beach Club's Deluxe Room that sleeps six.

  • Food

    Ale & Compass Restaurant, Yachtsman Steakhouse and The Market at Ale & Compass, plus Cape May Cafe and Beaches & Cream at Beach Club next door.

  • Pools

    Stormalong Bay is the shared main pool. There are also 3 leisure pools, including one at Beach Club Villas that official pages say Yacht Club guests may use.

Heads up: Official pages say the Skyliner will be closed for routine refurbishment January 24 through 30, 2027, and January 24 through February 2, 2028. Complimentary bus service will be available. Beach Club next door has visible exterior maintenance through late 2027.
