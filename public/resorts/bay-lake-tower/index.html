Bay Lake Tower Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Bay Lake Tower at Disney's Contemporary Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Deluxe Villa tower next to the Contemporary, linked by a skybridge. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio ran about $719 to $1,524 a night at full price, tax included, depending on the view. One-bedroom villas ran about $1,068 to $2,080, two-bedroom villas about $1,392 to $3,548, and Grand Villas about $3,170 to $6,084.

  • Getting to the parks

    Walking path or monorail to Magic Kingdom, via the Contemporary. Monorail to EPCOT with a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs. Boats also connect the Contemporary with Wilderness Lodge and Fort Wilderness.

  • Room size

    Deluxe Studios sleep 4 (a queen and a double-size pull-down). One-bedroom villas sleep 5. Two-bedroom villas sleep 9. Grand Villas sleep 12. Resort View can mean parking or an obstructed view; Preferred is Bay Lake; Theme Park is Magic Kingdom. Room sizes were not confirmed on a current official page we opened [verify].

  • Best for

    Families who want a walk or monorail to Magic Kingdom plus a kitchen, especially if a Theme Park View matters. A weaker pick if you need a fifth bed in a studio — Contemporary hotel rooms often sleep five, and these studios do not.

  • Food

    Shared with the Contemporary: Chef Mickey's, California Grill, Steakhouse 71 and Contempo Cafe. Disney's official site did not have a separate Bay Lake Tower dining page when we checked.

  • Pools

    Tower guests use the Contemporary Feature Pool, with a 17-foot slide, and the quieter Bay Lake Pool. Whether the tower has a separate pool of its own is still unverified [verify].

Heads up: A May 2026 planDisney answer described refurbishment work in the Contemporary atrium, on the Main Tower exterior and at Bay Lake Tower, expected to continue through late 2027. Views and some outdoor travel may be affected, and guests may see or hear work during the day. [verify current status]
