Saratoga Springs Resort: The Full Guide

Saratoga Springs is a large Disney Vacation Club resort across the water from Disney Springs, with a boat and a walking path over. Deluxe Studios sleep four; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas and Treehouse Villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. High Rock Spring and the Paddock Pool are the two feature pools.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort in the Disney Springs area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
Confirm the street address and check-in building on your reservation. The Carriage House is the main building. [verify current address]
Size
A large resort of several neighborhoods, inspired by upstate New York spa and horse-racing towns. Preferred locations are listed as closer to Disney Springs or to the Carriage House and dining. Treehouse Villas sit in their own wooded section.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 4; one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas and Treehouse Villas sleep 9; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studio: 1 queen and 1 double-size pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 twin-size pull-down. Two-bedroom: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 twin-size pull-down. Treehouse: 2 queens, 1 queen-size sleeper sofa, 1 bunk bed and 1 twin-size sleeper chair. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down.
Getting around
Sassagoula River Cruise boat to Disney Springs, plus a walking path. Buses to all four parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. This is not a Friendship boat to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios.
Food
The Artist's Palette, The Paddock Grill, The Turf Club Bar and Grill, On the Rocks Pool Bar and Backstretch Pool Bar, all listed on Disney's official Saratoga Springs dining page
Pools
High Rock Spring Pool (128-foot slide) and the Paddock Pool (146-foot slide), plus 3 leisure pools at the Treehouse Villas, Congress Park and The Grandstand

Who Saratoga Springs is best for

  • Families who want a Deluxe Villa near Disney Springs, with a boat and a walking path instead of only a bus.
  • Groups who specifically want a Treehouse Villa. Official 2026 listings put them at nine adults, in the woods, at about $1,226 to $2,332 a night at full price, tax included.
  • People who will use two feature pools. High Rock Spring and the Paddock Pool both have long slides and zero-depth entry.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio. Official listings cap Deluxe Studios at four adults (a queen and a double-size pull-down).
  • You book a standard (not Preferred) building at the far end of the resort and expect a short walk to the Carriage House or to Disney Springs. This property is large.
  • A short walk to a theme park is the main draw. All four parks are buses.

Pools and things to do

  • High Rock Spring Pool has waterfalls, zero-depth entry, a 128-foot slide built into the rocks, two whirlpool spas and a children's play area. On the Rocks is the pool bar here.
  • The Paddock Pool has a 146-foot slide that starts on a water tower, zero-depth entry, a horseshoe-shaped whirlpool spa and a children's aquatic play area with two small slides.
  • Three leisure pools, each with a whirlpool spa, are at the Treehouse Villas, Congress Park and The Grandstand. Official text says the latter two also have playgrounds and barbecue grills.

Pros

  • Boat and walking path to Disney Springs
  • Treehouse Villas, plus standard villas up to a Grand Villa
  • Two feature pools with long slides, plus three leisure pools
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Studios sleep four, not five
  • The resort is large; a far building adds a lot of walking or waiting
  • All four theme parks are buses
  • The boat does not go to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios

Booking and value tips

  • Preferred location is about proximity to Disney Springs or to the Carriage House, not a different room layout. If you will walk to Springs often, it may be worth the higher rate.
  • A Treehouse is a different layout than a standard two-bedroom, with bunks and a wooded setting. Compare both if you need nine people.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
