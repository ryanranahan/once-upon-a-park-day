Disney's Port Orleans Resort – Riverside

Southern riverside mansions and bayou-style buildings along a winding river.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Facts last checked [DATE]

Overview

Port Orleans Riverside is a Moderate resort split into two areas: grand Southern mansions and rustic bayou-style buildings, all along a winding river.

First-hand notes coming soon.

This guide is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take, photos and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Families of five who want a Moderate room with space, and anyone who likes a peaceful riverside setting.

Rooms

Standard rooms sleep four. Rooms in Alligator Bayou can sleep five with a trundle bed. The Royal Guest Rooms add princess-inspired decor.

Pools and things to do

The main pool, on Ol' Man Island, has a waterslide and a fishing hole. Several smaller pools are spread across the resort.

Where to eat

Boatwright's Dining Hall is the table-service restaurant and Riverside Mill Food Court is the quick-service option. The River Roost lounge often has live entertainment.

Getting to the parks

  • Buses to all four parks and Disney Springs
  • Boat to Disney Springs

Buses go to every park, and a boat goes to Disney Springs.

Pros and cons

Love

  • Rooms that sleep five
  • Pretty river views and walking paths
  • Boat to Disney Springs

Keep in mind

  • Large and spread out
  • Bus-only transportation to the parks
  • Some rooms are far from the main building

First-timer tips

  • Request a room near the main building or a bus stop.
  • Rent a bike or surrey to explore the river paths.
