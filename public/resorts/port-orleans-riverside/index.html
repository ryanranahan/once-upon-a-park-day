Port Orleans Riverside Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Port Orleans Resort – Riverside, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    A larger Moderate resort next to Port Orleans French Quarter. In 2026 a Standard Location room ran about $322 to $483 a night at full price, tax included. 5th Sleeper rooms ran about $361 to $541. Preferred rooms ran about $414 to $613, and Royal Guest Rooms ran about $408 to $579, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    Sassagoula River Cruise boat to Disney Springs and to Port Orleans French Quarter. Buses to all four theme parks and the water parks.

  • Room size

    Most rooms sleep 4, about 314 sq ft [verify], with 2 queen beds. Alligator Bayou 5th Sleeper rooms add a child-size pull-down bed (still listed as sleeping 4 adults). Royal Guest Rooms are a more themed category in Magnolia Bend. King rooms sleep 2.

  • Best for

    Families who want two queens, maybe a child-size pull-down bed, a table-service restaurant and a boat to Disney Springs. A weaker pick if you want a compact layout, or if construction through mid-2027 would bother you.

  • Food

    Riverside Mill Food Court and Boatwright's Dining Hall, plus Muddy Rivers at the main pool.

  • Pools

    Ol' Man Island is the main pool, with a 95-foot slide, a kiddie pool and a whirlpool spa. There are also 5 leisure pools. Guests can use Doubloon Lagoon at French Quarter too.

Heads up: Official pages say guest rooms and select public areas are under refurbishment through mid-2027. Guests may see or hear daytime construction, and alternate paths of travel may be in place.
