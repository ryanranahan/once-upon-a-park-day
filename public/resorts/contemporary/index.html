Contemporary Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Contemporary Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

Read the full guide

  • Category and price

    Deluxe resort in the Magic Kingdom area. This guide covers the hotel rooms in the Main Tower and Garden Wing. In 2026 a Garden Wing Resort View room ran about $638 to $1,137 a night at full price, tax included. Main Tower Theme Park View rooms ran about $960 to $1,518, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    A walking path and the resort monorail both go to Magic Kingdom. The monorail also goes to EPCOT after a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center. Boats connect this resort with Wilderness Lodge and Fort Wilderness. Buses serve Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs.

  • Room size

    Standard rooms are often listed around 400 to 437 sq ft [verify] and typically sleep up to 5 adults with 2 queen beds and a day bed. Garden Wing rooms sit next to the Main Tower. Main Tower rooms have Water View or Theme Park View. Club Level rooms include lounge access.

  • Best for

    Anyone who wants the shortest walk or monorail ride to Magic Kingdom, and families who need a standard room that can sleep five. It is a weaker pick if you were hoping to avoid construction in 2026 and 2027, or if a quieter, less busy hotel is the goal.

  • Food

    Chef Mickey's is the character buffet for breakfast and dinner. California Grill is the 15th-floor dinner restaurant, with a view of Magic Kingdom. Steakhouse 71 serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. Contempo Cafe is the quick-service spot on the Grand Canyon Concourse.

  • Pools

    The Feature Pool is the main pool, with a 17-foot waterslide. Bay Lake Pool is a quieter leisure pool. There are whirlpool spas, and cabanas may be available to rent.

Heads up: Refurbishment work is underway in the atrium, on the Main Tower exterior and at Bay Lake Tower, and it is expected to continue through late 2027. Views and some outdoor paths may be affected, and you may see or hear work during the day. [verify current status]
