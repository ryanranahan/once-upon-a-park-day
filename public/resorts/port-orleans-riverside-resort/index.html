Port Orleans Riverside Resort: The Full Guide

This is the larger side of Port Orleans, with Magnolia Bend and Alligator Bayou rooms, a table-service restaurant, and a boat to Disney Springs. Rooms are under refurbishment through mid-2027.

Who Port Orleans Riverside is best for

  • Families who want two queens, and maybe a child-size pull-down bed. The Alligator Bayou "5th Sleeper" rooms officially sleep four adults and add a child-size pull-down bed. In 2026 those rooms ran about $361 to $541 a night at full price, tax included.
  • Anyone who wants a boat to Disney Springs and a table-service restaurant (Boatwright's) on site.
  • People who like the idea of a Royal Guest Room in Magnolia Bend, at a higher nightly rate than a standard room.

Think twice if…

  • You want a compact resort. Riverside is larger than French Quarter, and a Standard Location room in Magnolia Bend can mean a walk to the food court and the boat dock. Preferred rooms are closer to the Sassagoula Steamboat Company, dining, shopping and transportation.
  • Construction would bother you. Official pages say guest rooms and select public areas are under refurbishment through mid-2027. Guests may see or hear daytime construction, and alternate paths may be in place.
  • Your parks are the main event and you want a Skyliner or monorail. Theme parks are buses from here.

Room types, sizes and beds

Rooms are in two neighborhoods. Official listings put Standard Location rooms in Magnolia Bend, and put the "5th Sleeper" rooms in Alligator Bayou. Preferred rooms can be in either neighborhood and are listed as close to the Sassagoula Steamboat Company, dining, shopping and transportation. Royal Guest Rooms are a more themed category in Magnolia Bend. King rooms can be in either neighborhood.

Getting to the parks

The Sassagoula River Cruise is the fun advantage here. Official water transportation listings show boat service from Riverside and French Quarter to Disney Springs, and the two resorts also connect to each other along the river. Theme parks and the water parks are buses.

Dining

  • Riverside Mill Food Court is the quick-service restaurant for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • Boatwright's Dining Hall is the table-service restaurant, listed for dinner in a Disney travel-agent overview. Confirm current hours before you go [verify hours still current].
  • Muddy Rivers is the pool bar at Ol' Man Island.
  • Port Orleans French Quarter, next door, has its own food court. Scat Cat's Club on that side is under refurbishment into late 2026 and early 2027.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

Pools and things to do

  • Ol' Man Island is the main pool, a 3.5-acre area reached by three wooden bridges. Official pages describe a 95-foot rustic waterslide, a whirlpool spa, a kiddie pool, the Muddy Rivers pool bar, and a campfire and movie on select nights.
  • Five leisure pools are spread through the resort, including pools in Alligator Bayou and Magnolia Bend.
  • Official pool pages also say Riverside guests can use Doubloon Lagoon at French Quarter.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Booking and value tips

  • Book a Standard Location room if four people is enough and you don't mind walking. In 2026 those rooms ran about $322 to $483 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates.
  • Book a 5th Sleeper room only if you will use the child-size pull-down bed. In 2026 those rooms ran about $361 to $541. Official listings still cap occupancy at four adults.
  • Pay for Preferred if the shorter walk to the food court and the boat dock matters, or for a Royal Guest Room only if the themed room is the point. In 2026 Preferred rooms ran about $414 to $613, and Royal Guest Rooms ran about $408 to $579.
  • Extra adults cost more. Moderate rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $25 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • If construction bothers you, wait until after mid-2027 or look at French Quarter, which did not have a matching room-refurbishment notice when we checked.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
