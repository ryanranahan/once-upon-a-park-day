Disney's Port Orleans Resort – French Quarter

A compact resort styled after the French Quarter of New Orleans.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Last updated November 2026

Overview

Port Orleans French Quarter is the smallest Moderate resort, styled after New Orleans with wrought-iron balconies and gas-style lamps. It feels quiet and easy to get around.

First-hand notes coming soon.

This guide is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take, photos and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Couples and small families who want a calm, compact Moderate resort.

Rooms

Standard rooms sleep four, and some royal-themed rooms sleep five. Most rooms are a short walk from the main building.

Pools and things to do

Doubloon Lagoon has a sea-serpent waterslide and a playful Mardi Gras feel.

Where to eat

Sassagoula Floatworks & Food Factory is the quick-service spot, famous for its beignets. For a sit-down meal, walk or take the boat to Riverside next door.

Getting to the parks

  • Buses to all four parks and Disney Springs
  • Boat to Disney Springs

Buses go to every park, and a boat goes to Disney Springs.

Pros and cons

Love

  • Compact and easy to walk
  • Charming New Orleans theming
  • Boat to Disney Springs

Keep in mind

  • Only one food court on site
  • Bus-only transportation to the parks
  • Fewer rooms means it can book up early

First-timer tips

  • Try the beignets at least once.
  • Take the boat to Disney Springs for dinner and shopping.
