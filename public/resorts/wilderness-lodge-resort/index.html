Wilderness Lodge: The Full Guide

This is a Deluxe lodge on Bay Lake, with a boat to Magic Kingdom and a waterfall running through the grounds. Most standard rooms sleep four, and the dining includes a character meal at Artist Point.

Who Wilderness Lodge is best for

  • Anyone who wants a boat to Magic Kingdom without the monorail crowds. The dock is on Bay Lake, and official listings also show boat service that connects this resort with the Contemporary and Fort Wilderness.
  • Families who like the setting as much as the parks. The lodge sits in the woods, with a waterfall in the rocks and a main pool built into the same landscape.
  • People who want table-service dining at the hotel. Whispering Canyon Cafe is the lively all-you-care-to-enjoy option, and Artist Point has a character dinner with Snow White.

Think twice if…

  • You need to sleep five in one standard room. These rooms sleep four, unless you book a Deluxe Club Level room or a villa.
  • A monorail from the hotel is the reason you booked a Magic Kingdom resort. The boat is the direct ride to the park. Everything else is a bus.
  • You are staying in late 2026 and need a guaranteed view. Disney has listed exterior work at the lodge and at Copper Creek through late 2026, and some views may be affected. [verify if still underway]

Dining

  • Whispering Canyon Cafe serves all-you-care-to-enjoy skillets at brunch and dinner. Disney listed brunch 7:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked. [verify hours still current]
  • Story Book Dining at Artist Point with Snow White is the character dinner, in a setting inspired by Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. Advance reservations are important.
  • Roaring Fork is the quick-service restaurant, listed at 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked, for breakfast, lunch and dinner. [verify hours still current]
  • Geyser Point Bar & Grill is the poolside restaurant and bar, listed at 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., with a deck looking toward Bay Lake. [verify hours still current]
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Artist Point books up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • Copper Creek Springs Pool is the main pool. It is heated, with a 67-foot slide built into the rocks, hot and cold whirlpool spas, and a children's play area. Geyser Point Bar & Grill is next to it.
  • Boulder Ridge Cove Pool at the Boulder Ridge Villas is a quieter zero-entry pool with a whirlpool spa and six shaded seating areas, available first come, first served.
  • Towels are free at both pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Boat to Magic Kingdom, plus boats that also serve the Contemporary and Fort Wilderness
  • A distinctive lodge setting, with a waterfall and a pool built into the rocks
  • Whispering Canyon Cafe, a character dinner at Artist Point, and Geyser Point on the water
  • A quieter feel than the monorail resorts, while still staying in the Magic Kingdom area

  • No monorail; EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom are buses

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Resort View room if the view is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran from $573 to $1,052 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest hotel rate at this resort.
  • Pay more for Water View or Fireworks View only if you will use it. Water View rooms ran about $641 to $1,073. Fireworks View is described as a partial view of the Magic Kingdom fireworks, and it costs more still.
  • If you need space for six, look at a Deluxe Club Level room before you jump to a villa. Those rooms are larger and include a sleeper sofa, plus lounge access.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • If you are staying in late 2026, read the exterior-renovation note before you book a specific view.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
