Art of Animation Resort: The Full Guide

Most rooms here are family suites, with two bathrooms and space for six. The Little Mermaid rooms are a standard Value room, and everyone shares a Skyliner station with Pop Century next door.

Who Art of Animation is best for

  • Families of five or six. The family suites sleep up to six, with two bathrooms and a kitchenette, at a Value resort price.
  • Anyone who wants the Skyliner at a Value rate. The station is on the Generation Gap Bridge, shared with Pop Century, and it goes to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios.
  • Smaller parties watching their budget. The Little Mermaid rooms cost less than the suites. They're a standard Value room, just a longer walk from Animation Hall.

Think twice if…

  • The pool or resort dining is the point of your trip. There's a food court, not a sit-down restaurant. The Big Blue Pool is huge, and it can feel crowded.
  • You want Extended Evening Hours. Value resort guests don't get them, and on those EPCOT nights the Skyliner route to Art of Animation shuts down early.
  • Your trip falls on Jan 24–30, 2027. The Skyliner is closed for refurbishment then, and buses fill in.
  • You're in a Little Mermaid room and you'd rather not walk. That section sits farthest from the lobby, food court and Skyliner.

Room types, sizes and beds

Art of Animation is really two products in one resort. The Little Mermaid section has standard Value rooms. The Cars, Finding Nemo and Lion King sections are family suites. Disney also sells a "Family Suites" category where the theme is assigned at check-in, usually at a slightly lower price than picking Finding Nemo yourself.

What's in the room

Family suites add a kitchenette with a microwave, plus a second bathroom and a separate sleeping area. Getting to the parks

Art of Animation has two free options: the Disney Skyliner gondola and Disney buses. The bus stop is outside Animation Hall. The Skyliner station sits on the Generation Gap Bridge and is shared with Pop Century.

Skyliner vs. bus: the honest version

  • Hours: the Skyliner opens an hour before Early Theme Park Entry at EPCOT or Hollywood Studios and closes 90 minutes after those parks close. Buses and other resort transportation start 45 minutes before park opening and run up to an hour after closing.
  • The EPCOT late-night catch: on nights when EPCOT has Extended Evening Hours, the Skyliner only runs between EPCOT and the Riviera. The route serving Caribbean Beach, Pop Century and Art of Animation isn't available, so plan on a bus.
  • Weather: it keeps running in light rain but stops for lightning or high wind. In summer storm season, keep the bus in mind as a backup.
  • Annual closure: Jan 24–30, 2027, with free buses instead.
  • Pop Century is next door. The Generation Gap Bridge links the two resorts, so you can walk over for Everything POP or hop a Pop Century bus if it arrives first.

Dining

  • Landscape of Flavors is the food court in Animation Hall. It serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. Disney's dining page listed hours of 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked [verify hours still current].
  • The Drop Off Pool Bar at the Big Blue Pool serves drinks and snacks.
  • Everything POP at Pop Century is a short walk across the Generation Gap Bridge if you want a second food court.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

Pools and things to do

  • The Big Blue Pool in the Finding Nemo courtyard is the main pool. Disney calls it the largest hotel pool at Walt Disney World and lists it at more than 308,527 gallons. There's a zero-entry side, the Schoolyard Sprayground for little kids, and Squirt's Righteous Reef playground if they want to stay dry. The Drop Off pool bar is next to it. It's also the busiest.
  • Cozy Cone Pool in the Cars courtyard is smaller, at 74,740 gallons, with cone-shaped cabanas around the edge.
  • Flippin' Fins Pool in the Little Mermaid courtyard holds 103,642 gallons and is usually a quieter option if you're staying in that section.
  • 24-hour self-service laundry rooms sit near all three pools.
  • Also on site: Pixel Play Arcade in Animation Hall, campfires, Movies Under the Stars on select nights, a second playground (The Boneyard) in the Lion King courtyard, and a 1.38-mile jogging trail around Hourglass Lake.

Booking and value tips

  • Book a Little Mermaid room if you only need space for four. In 2026 those rooms ran from $251 to $443 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That's the lowest rate at this resort.
  • If you need a suite, compare the "theme assigned at check-in" rate with a named section. Finding Nemo suites usually cost the most, because they're closest to the lobby and the main pool. In 2026, family suites ran about $519 to $992 a night at full price including tax.
  • Extra adults cost more. Rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $15 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Avoid Jan 24–30, 2027 if the Skyliner is why you're picking Art of Animation.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
