Caribbean Beach Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Caribbean Beach Resort, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

  • Category and price

    Moderate resort with a Skyliner station on property. In 2026 a Standard Location room ran about $310 to $523 a night at full price, tax included. Preferred Location rooms ran about $396 to $627, and King rooms ran about $374 to $590, depending on the dates.

  • Getting to the parks

    Skyliner gondolas to EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, and Pop Century / Art of Animation. Buses to Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs. Official pages say the Skyliner is closed January 24–30, 2027, and January 24–February 2, 2028, with buses filling in.

  • Room size

    Standard and Preferred rooms sleep 4, about 300 sq ft [verify], with 2 queen beds. Some rooms also have a child-size pull-down bed. King rooms sleep 2. Preferred rooms are closer to Centertown.

  • Best for

    Anyone who wants the Skyliner at a Moderate rate, and families who can live with a spread-out village layout. A weaker pick if Magic Kingdom is your main park, or if you don't want to walk.

  • Food

    Centertown Market and Spyglass Grill are quick service. Sebastian's Bistro is table service. Banana Cabana is the pool bar at Fuentes del Morro.

  • Pools

    Fuentes del Morro Pool is the main pool, with two slides (including one 102 feet long) and a 12-person spa. There are also 5 leisure pools, one in each village.

Heads up: Official pages say the Skyliner will be closed for routine refurbishment January 24 through 30, 2027, and January 24 through February 2, 2028. Complimentary bus service will be available.
