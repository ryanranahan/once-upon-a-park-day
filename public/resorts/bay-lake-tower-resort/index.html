Bay Lake Tower Resort: The Full Guide

Bay Lake Tower is the Disney Vacation Club tower next to the Contemporary, linked by a skybridge. Studios sleep four; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. You walk or monorail to Magic Kingdom from the Contemporary, and you share that hotel's restaurants and pools.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Contemporary Resort, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
4600 North World Drive, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same campus as the Contemporary; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
A high-rise tower next to the Contemporary, connected by a skybridge to the Main Tower monorail station and restaurants.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios sleep 4; one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas sleep 9; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studio: 1 queen and 1 double-size pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Two-bedroom: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 double-size pull-down.
Getting around
Walking path to Magic Kingdom; monorail to Magic Kingdom, and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center, from the Contemporary. Boats also connect the Contemporary with Wilderness Lodge and Fort Wilderness. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
Shared with the Contemporary: Chef Mickey's, California Grill, Steakhouse 71 and Contempo Cafe
Pools
Tower guests use the Contemporary Feature Pool (17-foot slide) and the quieter Bay Lake Pool. Whether the tower has a separate pool of its own is [verify].

Who Bay Lake Tower is best for

  • Families who want a walk or a monorail to Magic Kingdom, plus a kitchen. The skybridge puts you in the Contemporary's Main Tower for the monorail and the restaurants.
  • Groups who will pay for a Theme Park View. Official listings include Magic Kingdom views on studios through Grand Villas, at a higher rack rate than Resort or Preferred (Bay Lake) views.
  • People who already like the Contemporary and need more than a hotel room. The hotel rooms, which often sleep five, have their own page.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio. Official 2026 listings cap Deluxe Studios at four adults (a queen and a double-size pull-down). The Contemporary's standard hotel rooms are the ones that often sleep five.
  • A Resort View is just a polite name for parking. Official text describes some Resort Views as a parking area or an obstructed view. Read the category before you book.
  • You are sensitive to construction. A May 2026 planDisney answer described refurbishment work in the Contemporary atrium, on the Main Tower exterior and at Bay Lake Tower, expected to continue through late 2027. [verify current status]

Pools and things to do

  • Feature Pool at the Contemporary is the main pool, heated, with a 17-foot curving slide, marina views and a water-play area. Cabanas may be available to rent.
  • Bay Lake Pool is a quieter all-ages leisure pool, where official text says loud noise and splashing are gently discouraged.
  • Some secondary write-ups mention a pool at the tower itself. The official Contemporary pool page we opened describes the Feature Pool and Bay Lake Pool and does not name a separate tower pool. [verify whether Bay Lake Tower has its own pool]

Booking and value tips

  • If you need a fifth bed in one room, look at a one-bedroom villa, not a studio — or compare a Contemporary hotel room, which often sleeps five.
  • Read the view names. Preferred is Bay Lake. Theme Park is Magic Kingdom. Resort can be parking or an obstructed view.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
