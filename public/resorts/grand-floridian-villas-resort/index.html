Grand Floridian Villas Resort: The Full Guide

These are Disney Vacation Club villas next to the Grand Floridian, on the monorail and a boat to Magic Kingdom. Deluxe Studios and Resort Studios sleep five; one-bedroom villas sleep five; two-bedroom villas sleep nine; Grand Villas sleep twelve. You share the hotel's restaurants, Beach Pool and Courtyard Pool.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
4401 Floridian Way, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same campus as the Grand Floridian; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
The villas stand in their own building next to the hotel, in the same Victorian style.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios, Resort Studios and one-bedroom villas sleep 5; two-bedroom villas sleep 9; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Deluxe Studio: 1 queen, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Resort Studio: 2 queens and 1 day bed. One-bedroom: 1 king, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Two-bedroom: 1 king, 2 queens, 1 queen-size pull-down and 1 single pull-down. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down.
Getting around
Monorail to Magic Kingdom and to EPCOT via a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center; boat to Magic Kingdom; a walking path to Magic Kingdom is also listed. Buses to Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
Shared with the Grand Floridian, including Citricos, Narcoossee's, Grand Floridian Cafe and Gasparilla Island Grill. Confirm current hours on the hotel guide.
Pools
Beach Pool (181-foot slide, zero-depth entry, overlooking Seven Seas Lagoon) and the quieter Courtyard Pool behind the main building

Who the Grand Floridian Villas are best for

  • Families who want a monorail to Magic Kingdom and a kitchen, without moving to a different part of property. The villas share the hotel's monorail station, boat dock and restaurants.
  • Groups who need a fifth bed in a studio. Both the Deluxe Studio (pull-downs) and the Resort Studio (two queens and a day bed) are listed as sleeping five.
  • People who already like the Grand Floridian and want more space than a hotel room. This is not a quieter, cheaper resort across the lagoon; it is the same campus at villa prices.

Think twice if…

  • The nightly rate is the deciding factor. In 2026 a Deluxe Studio – Resort View ran about $832 to $1,370 a night at full price, tax included, and Grand Villas ran much higher.
  • You want a standalone villa resort with its own dining. There is no separate villas restaurant; you walk to the hotel.
  • You were picturing a standard hotel room. The photos on this page are villa interiors, not the hotel rooms covered in the Grand Floridian guide.

Room types, sizes and beds

This guide covers The Villas at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa. The hotel rooms at the Grand Floridian have their own page. Guests share restaurants, the monorail, the boat dock and both pools. Studios typically have a kitchenette; one-bedroom villas and larger typically have a full kitchen [verify current appliances]. There is no extra-adult charge up to the occupancy limit. Official 2026 listings split studios into Deluxe Studios (pull-down beds) and Resort Studios (two queens and a day bed).

Pools and things to do

  • Beach Pool is the main pool, 111,261 gallons, overlooking Seven Seas Lagoon. Official pages describe a 181-foot slide, cascading waterfalls, zero-depth entry and a lounge and bar on site.
  • Courtyard Pool is a quieter zero-entry leisure pool behind the main building, heated, about 5 feet at its deepest, with a whirlpool spa.

Pros

  • Monorail, boat and a walking path to Magic Kingdom
  • Studios that sleep five, including a Resort Studio with two queens and a day bed
  • Shared access to the Grand Floridian's restaurants and both pools
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Full-price studio rates start high, and Preferred or Theme Park views cost more
  • EPCOT is a monorail transfer at the TTC; the other parks are buses
  • No dedicated villas restaurant

Booking and value tips

  • Read the studio names carefully. A Deluxe Studio and a Resort Studio both sleep five, but the beds are different: pull-downs versus two queens and a day bed.
  • Theme Park View and Preferred View cost more. If you will spend most daylight hours in the parks, a Resort View may be enough.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices, tax included, from the 2026 published ranges above.
