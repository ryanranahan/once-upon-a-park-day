Beach Club Resort: The Full Guide

This Deluxe resort sits on Crescent Lake, a short walk from EPCOT's International Gateway, and shares the Stormalong Bay pool with Yacht Club next door. Many standard rooms sleep five.

Who Beach Club is best for

  • Anyone who wants to walk or take a boat to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios. The International Gateway is a short walk, and Friendship boats serve the crescent of hotels on the lake.
  • Families of five who don't want a suite. Official listings show many standard rooms sleeping up to five, usually with a day bed in addition to a king or two queens. In 2026 a Resort View room ran about $650 to $1,162 a night at full price, tax included.
  • People who will actually use Stormalong Bay. It is one of the most elaborate hotel pool complexes on property, shared with Yacht Club.

Think twice if…

  • Your parks are mostly Magic Kingdom or Animal Kingdom. Those are buses.
  • The nightly rate is the deciding factor. This is a Deluxe resort. Water View and Club Level rooms run higher still.
  • You are sensitive to exterior work. Official pages say Beach Club will have visible exterior maintenance through late 2027, which may affect some views and, at times, require alternate paths. Work is described as happening during daytime hours.

Room types, sizes and beds

This guide covers the hotel rooms at Beach Club. Beach Club Villas share the grounds and Stormalong Bay and have their own page. Official listings describe Resort View rooms as looking at gardens, a rooftop or parking, and Water View rooms as looking at Stormalong Bay, Crescent Lake or a quiet pool. Club Level rooms include lounge access. Deluxe Rooms are listed as larger than a standard room and sleep six.

Getting to the parks

This is one of the easier resorts for EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. You can walk to EPCOT through the International Gateway, or take a Friendship boat across Crescent Lake. Official water transportation listings support those boat routes from the Yacht and Beach Club area. The EPCOT Skyliner station is also a walk through the International Gateway. Buses cover Magic Kingdom, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs. Official pages say the Skyliner is closed January 24 through 30, 2027, and January 24 through February 2, 2028, with complimentary buses filling in.

Dining

  • Cape May Cafe is the character-dining restaurant at Beach Club, including Minnie's Beach Bash Breakfast. Confirm whether dinner is still offered, and current hours, before you book [verify current hours and whether dinner is still served].
  • Beaches & Cream Soda Shop is table service for lunch and dinner, with a soda-fountain menu.
  • Next door at Yacht Club: Ale & Compass Restaurant (breakfast, lunch and dinner), Yachtsman Steakhouse (Signature dinner), and The Market at Ale & Compass (quick service).
  • The BoardWalk restaurants are a short walk around the lake.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Cape May Cafe and Yachtsman Steakhouse can book up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • Stormalong Bay is the shared main pool for Beach Club, Yacht Club and Beach Club Villas. Official pages describe a large, varied complex rather than a single rectangle of water, with both more active and quieter areas.
  • Three leisure pools sit around the Yacht and Beach Club grounds, including one at Beach Club Villas that official pages say Yacht Club guests may use. Official pages also mention whirlpool spas at the pools.
  • The Beach Club leisure pool is described as sitting in a garden area facing Crescent Lake. The Yacht Club leisure pool is in its own garden area.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Resort View room if the water is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran about $650 to $1,162 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. Water View rooms ran about $772 to $1,231.
  • Confirm the bed setup if five people need to sleep comfortably. Official listings allow several combinations, and not every room has a day bed.
  • A Deluxe Room sleeps six and is listed as larger than a standard room. In 2026 those rooms ran about $836 to $1,318. Compare that with a 1-Bedroom villa next door at Beach Club Villas.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
