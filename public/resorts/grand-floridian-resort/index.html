Grand Floridian Resort: The Full Guide

This is Disney's flagship Deluxe resort on the Seven Seas Lagoon, with a monorail and a boat to Magic Kingdom. Most standard rooms sleep five, and the dining list is one of the longest on property.

Who the Grand Floridian is best for

  • Anyone who wants an easy trip to Magic Kingdom. You can ride the resort monorail or take the boat. Theme Park View rooms look toward the park.
  • Families of five who don't want a suite. Most standard rooms sleep five, and they are usually in the outer buildings, which can feel a little quieter than the main building.
  • People who want a lot of dining without leaving the hotel. There is a character buffet, several table-service restaurants, a cafe, a quick-service grill and two lounges, plus a spa on site.

Think twice if…

  • The nightly rate is the deciding factor. This is a Deluxe resort at the high end. In 2026, a Resort View room ran about $832 to $1,370 a night at full price, tax included.
  • Your parks are mostly EPCOT or Hollywood Studios. EPCOT takes a monorail change at the TTC. Hollywood Studios is a bus.
  • You want a completely quiet arrival. Renovations in select areas, including the porte cochere, continue through early 2027. Disney says you may see or hear work during the day or need to take another path.

Getting to the parks

The monorail station and the boat dock are the two big advantages here. The boat is the Gold Launch, and official water transportation listings also show it serving the Polynesian Village on the way to Magic Kingdom. Buses leave for the parks the monorail and boat don't cover.

Dining

  • 1900 Park Fare is the character buffet, branded as Wish Makers Enchanted Dining. Breakfast is listed at 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and dinner at 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Disney says characters may include Cinderella, Tiana, Aladdin and Mirabel, and that appearances can change. [verify hours still current]
  • Grand Floridian Cafe is table service for breakfast, lunch and dinner. It reopened in November 2026 after a mid-year refurbishment. Dinner was listed at 5:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. when we checked. [verify hours still current]
  • Cítricos and Narcoossee's are table-service restaurants. Narcoossee's sits on the water, by the boat dock. Victoria & Albert's is the fine-dining restaurant; book well ahead if that is part of your trip.
  • Gasparilla Island Grill is the quick-service restaurant.
  • Enchanted Rose and Garden View Lounge are the lounges. Garden View is known for afternoon tea.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. 1900 Park Fare and Victoria & Albert's book up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • Beach Pool is the main pool. Disney lists it at 111,261 gallons, overlooking the Seven Seas Lagoon, with a 181-foot slide, cascading waterfalls, zero-depth entry, a walking bridge, a lounge and bar, and a splash area for little kids. Cabanas can be rented. This is the busy one.
  • Courtyard Pool is a quieter, heated zero-entry leisure pool behind the main building. It goes down to nearly 5 feet and has a whirlpool spa next to it.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Life jackets are available at The Captain's Shipyard. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.
  • There is also a spa on site.

  • Theme Park View rooms look toward Magic Kingdom

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Resort View room if the view is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran from $832 to $1,370 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest hotel rate at this resort.
  • Pay more for Water View or Theme Park View only if you will use it. Water View rooms ran about $864 to $1,506. Theme Park View was higher still. Confirm you are in an outer building if that is what you booked, and think about the walk.
  • Club Level is a different product. You are paying for the lounge, and some Club rooms are larger. Compare that cost with eating some of those meals at Grand Floridian Cafe or Gasparilla.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
