Animal Kingdom Lodge: The Full Guide

Many rooms here look out over savannas with giraffes and zebras just outside the balcony. It's a Deluxe resort in the Animal Kingdom area, and buses are the way to every park.

Who Animal Kingdom Lodge is best for

  • Anyone who wants animals right outside the room. Savanna View rooms look over one of the grazing areas. Even if you book a Resort View or Water View room, you can still walk out to the viewing spots around the lobby and the grounds.
  • Families who like having real restaurants at the hotel. Boma and Jiko are at Jambo House, and Sanaa is a short walk at Kidani Village. You are not limited to a food court.
  • Parties of four who don't need a fifth bed. Standard rooms sleep four. They are on the smaller side for a Deluxe resort, at about 344 sq ft [verify].

Think twice if…

  • You need to sleep five in one hotel room. A standard room here does not have a day bed. You would need a suite, or a villa next door.
  • A short trip to Magic Kingdom is the point of the stay. This is a bus-only resort. Animal Kingdom is the closest park, and the other three are a longer bus ride.
  • You are staying between now and January 2027. Jambo House guest rooms are under renovation from May 2026 through January 2027. Disney says you may see or hear construction, and that most amenities remain available.

Getting to the parks

Buses are the only free Disney transportation from Animal Kingdom Lodge. They run to all four theme parks, the water parks and Disney Springs. The official bus page says resort buses start 45 minutes before park opening and run up to an hour after closing, which is the standard window for this kind of service [verify those hours still apply here].

Dining

  • Boma – Flavors of Africa is the buffet, for breakfast and dinner. Disney listed breakfast 7:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. when we checked. [verify hours still current]
  • Jiko – The Cooking Place is the table-service dinner restaurant, listed at 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. It's a Disney Signature Dining location, and Disney says it does not take same-day bookings. [verify hours still current]
  • The Mara is the quick-service restaurant, listed at 6:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked. [verify hours still current]
  • Sanaa is table service at Kidani Village, a short walk from Jambo House.
  • Both Uzima Springs Pool and Samawati Springs Pool have a lounge and bar for drinks and snacks.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Jiko and Boma book up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • Uzima Springs Pool at Jambo House is the main pool. Disney lists it at 11,000 square feet and 234,980 gallons, facing Uzima Savanna, with a 67-foot slide, zero-depth entry, and a lounge and bar nearby.
  • Samawati Springs Pool at Kidani Village is a short walk away. Official listings say it is 4,700 square feet and 118,138 gallons, with a 128-foot slide and zero-depth entry. Uwanja Camp, next to that pool, is a splash area with zones aimed at younger and older kids.
  • Towels are free at the pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.
  • The other big activity is the animals. Official pages say more than 200 hoofed animals and birds roam four savannas around the lodge. You can watch from a Savanna View balcony or from the public viewing areas.

Pros and cons

  • Savanna views, with animals just outside many rooms

  • Uzima Springs Pool looks out at a savanna
  • Closest Deluxe resort to Animal Kingdom

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Resort View room if the balcony view is not the point. In 2026 those rooms ran from $508 to $915 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest hotel rate at this resort.
  • Pay for Savanna View if watching the animals is why you booked. Savanna View rooms ran about $731 to $1,227. Water View rooms, which look at the pool, ran about $556 to $976.
  • Don't book a standard room if you need space for five. These rooms sleep four. A one-bedroom suite or a villa is the next step up.
  • Club Level is a different product. You are paying for the lounge, usually with a savanna view. Compare that cost with breakfast at Boma or The Mara.
  • Extra adults cost more. Deluxe rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $35 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • If you are staying through January 2027, read the renovation note before you book a specific view or floor.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
