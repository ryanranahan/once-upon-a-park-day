Who Pop Century is best for

  • Budget-minded families who want EPCOT and Hollywood Studios days. It's a Value resort with its own Skyliner station, which it shares with Art of Animation.
  • Two adults with one or two young kids. The pull-down bed folds up into a table, so the room opens up during the day.
  • Park-all-day travelers who mostly use the room to sleep and shower.

Think twice if…

  • You're four adults. At about 260 sq ft with one bathroom, that's roughly 65 sq ft each [verify size].
  • The pool or resort dining is the point of your trip. There's a food court, not a sit-down restaurant, and none of the pools has a hot tub.
  • You want Extended Evening Hours. Value resort guests don't get them, and on those EPCOT nights the Skyliner route to Pop Century shuts down early.
  • Your trip falls on Jan 24–30, 2027. The Skyliner is closed for refurbishment then, and buses fill in.

Room types, sizes and beds

Pop Century has no suites. Every standard room has the same layout and size. Disney sells it in four categories, and the price difference pays for location and view, not extra space. The doors open onto outdoor walkways, so "view" mostly tells you which part of the resort you're in.

What's in the room

Disney lists a beverage cooler, coffee machine, hairdryer, in-room safe, free Wi-Fi, iron and ironing board, and a phone with voicemail. One independent guide says there's no microwave [verify]. A soft-goods refresh finished in 2026 and added color: wood-toned floors with a geometric inset, blue accents, new curtains and patterned chairs.

Accessible rooms exist, but we haven't confirmed their layouts or bed options yet [verify].

Which buildings are "Preferred"?

Picture the Magic lists the Preferred buildings as 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10, in the '50s, '60s and '70s sections [verify with Disney]. If you're driving, a Standard room can actually be closer to your car.

Getting to the parks

Pop Century has two free options: the Disney Skyliner gondola and Disney buses. The bus stop is outside Classic Hall. The Skyliner station is shared with Art of Animation.

Skyliner vs. bus: the honest version

  • Hours: the Skyliner opens an hour before Early Theme Park Entry at EPCOT or Hollywood Studios and closes 90 minutes after those parks close. Buses and other resort transportation start 45 minutes before park opening and run up to an hour after closing.
  • The EPCOT late-night catch: on nights when EPCOT has Extended Evening Hours, the Skyliner only runs between EPCOT and the Riviera. The route serving Caribbean Beach, Pop Century and Art of Animation isn't available, so plan on a bus.
  • Weather: it keeps running in light rain but stops for lightning or high wind. In summer storm season, keep the bus in mind as a backup.
  • Annual closure: Jan 24–30, 2027, with free buses instead.
  • Art of Animation is next door. The Generation Gap Bridge links the two resorts. One reviewer took an Art of Animation bus back from Magic Kingdom when it arrived first and walked over.

Dining

  • Everything POP Shopping & Dining is the food court in Classic Hall. It serves breakfast, lunch and dinner from quick-service stations. Disney lists 7 stations with food like waffles, pasta, flatbread and salads. A new coffee shop was added in the recent refresh.
  • Petals Pool Bar at the Hippy Dippy Pool serves cocktails, beer and sangria.
  • Art of Animation's food court, Landscape of Flavors, is a walk across the bridge.
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival.

The tie-dyed cheesecake is the food court's best-known dessert [verify still on the menu].

Pools and things to do

  • Hippy Dippy Pool is the main pool. It's flower-shaped with flower water jets, plus a kiddie area with a pop-jet fountain. It's also the busiest.
  • Bowling Pool ('50s) and Computer Pool ('90s) are smaller and usually quieter.
  • 24-hour self-service laundry is near all three pools.
  • Also on site: campfires, Movie Under the Stars on select nights, a one-mile jogging trail, a playground in the '70s section, and Fast Forward Arcade in Classic Hall. Fishing isn't allowed on Hourglass Lake.

Booking and value tips

  • Book the cheapest category if you don't mind walking. All four categories are the same room. In 2026, a Standard Room ran from $213 to $404 a night at full price including tax, depending on dates. Preferred Pool View ran up to $459.
  • Extra adults cost more. Rates assume two adults. Each extra adult adds $15 a night plus tax, and kids under 18 are free.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for Pop Century discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Avoid Jan 24–30, 2027 if the Skyliner is why you're picking Pop Century.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
