Riviera: The Full Guide

This is a Deluxe Villa resort in the EPCOT area, with its own Skyliner station and a rooftop restaurant. Tower Studios sleep two, Deluxe Studios sleep five, and the dining includes a character breakfast and a Signature dinner at Topolino's Terrace.

Who Riviera is best for

  • Anyone who wants a Skyliner ride to EPCOT or Hollywood Studios. The station is at the resort, and the ride to EPCOT's International Gateway is direct.
  • Couples who can live in a smaller room, or families who need a studio that sleeps five. A Tower Studio is built for two. A Deluxe Studio adds a queen bed and a single pull-down.
  • People who want character breakfast and a Signature dinner in the same rooftop restaurant. Topolino's Terrace does both, and the dinner does not include characters.

Think twice if…

  • You need a standard hotel room rather than a villa-style studio. This is a Deluxe Villa resort. Even the smallest rooms use a pull-down bed, and the cash rates are in Deluxe territory.
  • Magic Kingdom is your main park. That trip is a bus, not a Skyliner or monorail.
  • You are staying the week of January 24 through 30, 2027, and the Skyliner was the reason you booked here. Official pages say the Skyliner will close that week for refurbishment, with buses running instead.

Getting to the parks

The Skyliner is the reason many people book here. The station is at the resort, and the line runs directly to EPCOT's International Gateway. A trip to Hollywood Studios uses the same system, with a transfer at Caribbean Beach. Buses cover the parks the Skyliner doesn't reach.

Dining

  • Topolino's Terrace – Flavors of the Riviera is the rooftop restaurant. Breakfast is a character meal, Breakfast à la Art with Mickey & Friends; Disney listed 7:30 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. when we checked, and you may see Mickey, Minnie, Donald and Daisy in outfits unique to this restaurant. Dinner is a Signature dining experience with French- and Italian-inspired dishes, listed at 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. Characters do not appear at dinner, and there is a dress code. Advance reservations are important. [verify hours still current]
  • Primo Piatto is the quick-service restaurant, listed at 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked, for casual meals and snacks. [verify hours still current]
  • Le Petit Cafe is the lobby cafe. Independent listings have had it open 6:30 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.; we have not confirmed those hours on a fully loaded official page in this pass. [verify hours]
  • Bar Riva is the open-air pool bar, listed at 11:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked, with drinks and casual food. [verify hours still current]
  • You can use mobile ordering in the My Disney Experience app at the quick-service spots.
  • Park dining reservations: Disney resort guests can book their whole stay (up to 10 nights) starting 60 days before arrival. Topolino's Terrace books up, so plan ahead.

Pools and things to do

  • The Riviera Pool is the family pool, with a slide that winds around a column tower and S'il Vous Play, an interactive water-play area for kids.
  • Beau Soleil Pool is the quieter pool, in a calmer corner of the property.
  • Towels are free at both pools. Hours and lifeguard coverage vary and can change.

Booking and value tips

  • Start with a Tower Studio if there are only two of you and you can live with a pull-down bed. In 2026 those rooms ran from $519 to $966 a night at full cash price including tax, depending on dates. That is the lowest cash rate at this resort.
  • Book a Deluxe Studio if you need to sleep more than two, or if you want a traditional bed in the room. Resort View studios ran about $742 to $1,290. Preferred View costs more and looks at the courtyard, the lake, the pool or the Skyliner.
  • This is also a Disney Vacation Club resort. Some guests stay here on points or by renting points, which can change the value math. We are quoting cash rates in this guide.
  • If you are staying the last week of January 2027, plan on buses instead of the Skyliner.
  • Check Disney's own offers page first. Disney points to its special offers page for discounts. Our deals hub will track current ones.
  • Lightning Lane head start: Disney resort guests can buy Lightning Lane passes up to 7 days before their stay.
