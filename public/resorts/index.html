Walt Disney World

Value

The lowest-priced Disney-owned hotels. Big, brightly themed resorts with food courts instead of table-service restaurants, and buses or the Skyliner to the parks.

Animal Kingdom area All-Star Movies Bright, playful buildings decorated with giant icons from classic Disney films. Animal Kingdom area All-Star Music Music-themed buildings with oversized instruments, plus family suites for bigger groups. Animal Kingdom area All-Star Sports Sports-themed buildings with supersized balls, helmets and courts. Wide World of Sports area Pop Century A walk through 20th-century pop culture, decade by decade, across the lake from Art of Animation. Wide World of Sports area Art of Animation Themed to animated films, with most rooms set up as family suites.

Moderate

A step up in theming, pools and dining, with more spread-out, quieter grounds. A middle ground between Value and Deluxe.

Epcot area Caribbean Beach Colorful Caribbean island villages around a lake, and the central hub of the Skyliner. Animal Kingdom area Coronado Springs Spanish and Mexican-inspired buildings around a large lake, including the Gran Destino Tower. Disney Springs area Port Orleans French Quarter A compact resort styled after the French Quarter of New Orleans. Disney Springs area Port Orleans Riverside Southern riverside mansions and bayou-style buildings along a winding river.

Deluxe

The best locations, closest to the parks, with the most restaurants, the biggest pools and more ways to get around without a bus.

Animal Kingdom area Animal Kingdom Lodge An African-inspired lodge where many rooms look out over savannas with live animals. Epcot area Beach Club A breezy New England seaside hotel, sharing a large sand-bottom pool area with the Yacht Club. Epcot area BoardWalk Inn A turn-of-the-century Atlantic seaboard boardwalk on Crescent Lake, with shops and restaurants outside the door. Magic Kingdom area Contemporary A modern A-frame tower that the monorail runs right through, a short walk from Magic Kingdom. Magic Kingdom area Grand Floridian A Victorian-style seaside hotel on the Seven Seas Lagoon, and Walt Disney World's flagship resort. Magic Kingdom area Polynesian Village A South Pacific island resort on the Seven Seas Lagoon, looking across the water to Magic Kingdom. Magic Kingdom area Wilderness Lodge A grand national park lodge with a towering lobby, set in the woods on Bay Lake. Epcot area Yacht Club A polished New England yacht club, sharing a large sand-bottom pool area with the Beach Club.

Deluxe Villas

Disney Vacation Club resorts. You do not need to be a member to stay. Villas range from studios with a kitchenette to multi-bedroom villas with a full kitchen, which suits bigger families and longer stays.

Magic Kingdom area Bay Lake Tower A Disney Vacation Club tower next to the Contemporary, linked to it by a skybridge. Magic Kingdom area Boulder Ridge Villas Disney Vacation Club villas inside the Wilderness Lodge, styled after the railroad era of the American West. Magic Kingdom area Copper Creek Villas & Cabins Disney Vacation Club villas inside the Wilderness Lodge, plus standalone cabins along Bay Lake. Disney Springs area Old Key West A laid-back Key West-style resort with roomy villas, near Disney Springs. Disney Springs area Saratoga Springs A large resort inspired by the spa and horse-racing towns of upstate New York, across the lake from Disney Springs. Epcot area Riviera An elegant European Riviera-style resort with its own Skyliner station. Magic Kingdom area Polynesian Villas & Bungalows Disney Vacation Club villas at the Polynesian, plus bungalows that sit over the Seven Seas Lagoon. Magic Kingdom area Island Tower The newest Disney Vacation Club tower at the Polynesian, on the Seven Seas Lagoon. Magic Kingdom area Grand Floridian Villas Disney Vacation Club villas in the same Victorian style as the Grand Floridian next door. Animal Kingdom area Kidani Village Disney Vacation Club villas next to Animal Kingdom Lodge, with their own savanna views. Epcot area Beach Club Villas Disney Vacation Club villas attached to the Beach Club, with access to the same pool area. Epcot area BoardWalk Villas Disney Vacation Club villas on the BoardWalk, sharing the building with the BoardWalk Inn. Magic Kingdom area Cabins at Fort Wilderness Disney Vacation Club cabins tucked into the woods at Fort Wilderness.

Campground

Bring a tent or RV and stay in the woods, with a boat to Magic Kingdom.

Magic Kingdom area Fort Wilderness Campground Wooded campsites for tents and RVs on Bay Lake, with trails, horses and campfire evenings.

Hotels on Disney property that Disney does not run, such as the Swan, Dolphin and Swan Reserve, Shades of Green, and the Disney Springs area hotels, will get their own guides later.
