Fort Wilderness Campground Resort: Quick Facts

The short version of Disney's Fort Wilderness Resort & Campground, in six answers. For rooms, transport times and booking tips, read the full guide.

By Ryan · Last verified Nov 2026

  • Category and price

    Campground on Bay Lake, not a hotel. In 2026 a tent or pop-up site ran about $82 to $222 a night at full price, tax included. Full Hook-Up ran about $125 to $271, Preferred about $138 to $297, Premium about $148 to $313, and Premium Meadow about $156 to $348.

  • Getting to the parks

    Internal bus around the resort, then a boat to Magic Kingdom. Official water-transportation listings also show boats that connect Fort Wilderness with the Contemporary and Wilderness Lodge. Buses to EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs, usually after an internal bus.

  • Room size

    Campsites, not hotel rooms. Every 2026 category is listed as sleeping up to 8 adults, with no extra-adult charge up to that limit. Pad sizes range from 10 by 25 feet on tent or pop-up sites to 18 by 60 feet on Premium and Premium Meadow sites. Preferred sites are listed as closer to the marina.

  • Best for

    People who already camp or travel with an RV and want official Disney resort transportation, including a boat to Magic Kingdom. A weaker pick if you want a hotel room — look at the cabins page instead — or if you dislike internal buses.

  • Food

    Trail's End Restaurant, listed on Disney's official page with hours of 7:30 a.m. to 9:45 p.m. when we checked, and Meadow Snack Bar, an official quick-service kiosk. Confirm any other campground food stands before you go [verify the full current dining list].

  • Pools

    The Meadow Swimmin' Pool area is the main pool complex. Official pool pages we tried did not load a full description [verify current pool names and features].
