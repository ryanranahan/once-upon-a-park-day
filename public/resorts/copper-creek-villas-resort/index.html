Copper Creek Villas & Cabins Resort: The Full Guide

These are Disney Vacation Club villas and lakefront cabins at Wilderness Lodge. Studios and one-bedroom villas sleep four; two-bedroom villas and cabins sleep eight; Grand Villas sleep twelve. You use the same boat to Magic Kingdom, and the same restaurants and pools, as the lodge.

At a glance

Resort type
Deluxe Villa resort at Disney's Wilderness Lodge, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area. Disney Vacation Club inventory is mixed with cash bookings.
Address
901 Timberline Drive, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830 (same campus as Wilderness Lodge; confirm check-in location on your reservation)
Size
Villas are inside and around the lodge. The two-bedroom cabins sit on their own along Bay Lake.
Rooms
Deluxe Studios and one-bedroom villas sleep 4; two-bedroom villas and two-bedroom cabins sleep 8; three-bedroom Grand Villas sleep 12. A single child under 3 in a crib does not count toward the maximum.
Beds
Studios: 1 queen and 1 queen-size pull-down. One-bedroom: 1 king and 1 queen-size pull-down. Two-bedroom villa: 1 king, 2 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down. Cabins: 1 king, 1 queen, 1 queen-size sleeper sofa, 1 single pull-down and 1 twin-size sleeper chair. Grand Villa: 1 king, 4 queens and 1 queen-size pull-down.
Getting around
Boat to Magic Kingdom; boats also connect to the Contemporary and Fort Wilderness. Buses to EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, the water parks and Disney Springs
Food
Whispering Canyon Cafe, Story Book Dining at Artist Point, Roaring Fork and Geyser Point Bar & Grill, shared with the lodge
Pools
Copper Creek Springs Pool (main pool, with a 67-foot slide) and Boulder Ridge Cove Pool at the villas

Who Copper Creek is best for

  • Families who want a kitchen and more space than a Wilderness Lodge hotel room, without leaving the boat to Magic Kingdom. The villas share the lodge's lobby, restaurants and dock.
  • Groups who can use a two-bedroom villa or a cabin. Both sleep eight. Cabins sit on Bay Lake and are priced in a different range than the villas in the lodge.
  • People who already like Wilderness Lodge and want to stay on the same grounds. This is not a separate hotel on the other side of property.

Think twice if…

  • You need a fifth bed in a studio or a one-bedroom. Official 2026 listings cap both at four adults. Boulder Ridge studios, by comparison, are listed as sleeping five.
  • A monorail from the building is the main reason you want a Magic Kingdom-area resort. This is a boat ride, plus a walk from some rooms to the dock.
  • You wanted a cabin but are comparing it with a two-bedroom villa on price alone. In 2026 a two-bedroom cabin ran about $2,823 to $5,440 a night at full price, tax included, which is well above the two-bedroom villa range of about $1,444 to $2,829.

Dining

  • Whispering Canyon Cafe serves all-you-care-to-enjoy skillets at brunch and dinner. Disney listed brunch 7:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and dinner 5:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. when we checked the lodge guide sources. [verify hours still current]
  • Story Book Dining at Artist Point is the character dinner with Snow White, in the lodge.
  • Roaring Fork is the quick-service restaurant, listed at 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. when we checked. [verify hours still current]
  • Geyser Point Bar & Grill is the poolside restaurant and bar, listed at 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., with a deck looking toward Bay Lake. [verify hours still current]

Pools and things to do

  • Copper Creek Springs Pool is the main pool. It is heated, with a 67-foot slide built into the rocks, hot and cold whirlpool spas, and a children's play area. Geyser Point Bar & Grill is next to it.
  • Boulder Ridge Cove Pool is a quieter zero-entry pool with a whirlpool spa, at the Boulder Ridge Villas. Lodge and Copper Creek guests can use it.

Pros

  • Same boat to Magic Kingdom as the lodge
  • Studios through Grand Villas, plus lakefront cabins that sleep eight
  • Shared dining, including a character dinner at Artist Point
  • No extra-adult charge up to the listed occupancy

Cons

  • Studios and one-bedroom villas sleep four, not five
  • Cabins are expensive at full price compared with a two-bedroom villa
  • Buses to the other parks, and a walk from some units to the dock
  • Disney has listed exterior work at the lodge and Copper Creek through late 2026 [verify if this work is still underway]

Booking and value tips

  • Compare a Copper Creek one-bedroom with a Boulder Ridge studio if you need a fifth bed. Copper Creek's one-bedroom is listed as sleeping four; Boulder Ridge studios are listed as sleeping five.
  • Don't assume a cabin is just a fancier two-bedroom villa. The bed layout and the Bay Lake setting are different, and so is the price.
  • Disney Vacation Club members book with points; cash rates are full rack prices. Discounts can exist, but the ranges above are the published 2026 rack rates, tax included.
