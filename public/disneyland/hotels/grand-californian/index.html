Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa

A California Craftsman lodge with redwood and Monterey pine details, right beside Disney California Adventure.

Researched guide First-hand notes coming soon · Facts last checked November 2026

Overview

The Grand Californian is styled after the Arts and Crafts movement, with stone, timber and redwood touches throughout. Its biggest draw is location: the hotel has its own gate into Disney California Adventure, and Disneyland Park and Downtown Disney are a short walk away.

First-hand notes coming soon.

This guide is put together from research, not a stay. A first-hand take, photos and tips will be added later. Check details with the hotel before you book.

Who it suits

Families and couples who want the most convenient spot at Disneyland Resort and a quieter, lodge-style feel.

Pools and things to do

There are three pools, the Fountain, Redwood and Mariposa pools, along with two whirlpools and the Redwood Tree waterslide. GCH Craftsman Bar sits by the pool. Cabanas and daybeds can be rented.

Where to eat

Napa Rose is the signature restaurant. Storytellers Cafe serves character meals, and the hotel also hosts Disney Princess Breakfast Adventures. GCH Craftsman Grill is the grab-and-go spot, and Hearthstone Lounge is the bar. Tenaya Stone Spa is on site too.

Club-level guests get the Sequoia Perch concierge lounge.

Getting to the parks

  • Its own hotel entrance into Disney California Adventure (an active hotel reservation is required)
  • A walk to Disneyland Park and Downtown Disney

Pros and cons

Love

  • Its own entrance into Disney California Adventure
  • A walk to everything, with no buses
  • A spa and three pools on site

Keep in mind

  • Usually the priciest of the Disneyland hotels
  • The DCA gate only works with an active hotel reservation

First-timer tips

  • Use the hotel's DCA entrance on the way in; it skips the walk around to the main gate.
  • If you want a character meal, look at Storytellers Cafe.
