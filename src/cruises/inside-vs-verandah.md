## The four main categories

- **Inside:** no window to the outside, and the lowest price. On the **Disney Dream** and **Disney Fantasy**, many inside rooms have a **Magical Porthole**, a screen that shows a live view from outside the ship.
- **Oceanview:** a window or porthole you can't open. Natural light without the verandah price.
- **Verandah:** your own private balcony. The most popular choice, and there are a few types.
- **Concierge:** the top tier, with larger rooms and suites, a concierge lounge and earlier booking windows. The largest suites sleep up to 7.

## Inside vs. verandah, side by side

| | Inside | Verandah |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Lowest | Higher |
| **Light** | None (or a Magical Porthole on Dream and Fantasy) | Floor-to-ceiling glass and a balcony |
| **Best for** | People who are rarely in the room | Quiet mornings, naps, and watching the sea |
| **Sleeping** | Very dark, great for sleeping in | You can see the sunrise |

## Which to book

- **Book an inside room** if you'll be at the pool, the kids clubs and the shows all day. You'll save money for port adventures or a Palo dinner.
- **Book a verandah** if you'll want downtime in your room, if you have little ones who nap, or if you're sailing somewhere scenic like Alaska.
- **Try an oceanview** if you want natural light without paying for a balcony.

## Good to know

- Many Disney staterooms have a **split bathroom**, with the toilet separate from the shower and sink. It's a big help for families getting ready at the same time.
- On the Disney Dream, a standard inside room is about **169 square feet**, and a deluxe inside room is about **204 square feet**.
