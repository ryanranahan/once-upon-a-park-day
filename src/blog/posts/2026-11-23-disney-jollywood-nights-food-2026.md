Disney Jollywood Nights is Hollywood Studios' after-hours holiday party, and food is a big part of why people go. Disney just shared a peek at how this year's menu came together, so here's a plain-English look at what's on it and how to decide whether it belongs on your trip.

## What is Disney Jollywood Nights?

It's a **separately ticketed evening event** at Disney's Hollywood Studios, with holiday entertainment, characters you don't usually see, and a menu that's only served that night. It's a different ticket from your regular park ticket. Disney World's event page has this year's dates and prices, and those change from night to night, so check there before you plan around it.

## How big is the menu this year?

Disney says there are **almost 80 event-exclusive foods and drinks**, and **nearly 30 are new**. Chef Frank Vega, who has been cooking for Disney for more than 20 years, led the menu, and Disney says the culinary teams pitched about 50 new ideas after last year's event.

## What are the new things worth trying?

Here's what stood out to us from Disney's list, with where to find each one:

- **Yuletide Ham Popover:** honey-glazed ham, mashed potatoes, gravy and cranberry chutney baked into a popover. Basically a holiday dinner you can hold.
- **Churro Crafted Soda:** churro oat milk with Coca-Cola, gingerbread syrup and a mini churro on top.
- **Cookie Butter Croissant Beignets** at Epic Eats. Disney says you can watch them being fried, which is half the fun.
- **A TWIX on a Classic Pie** at Fairfax Fare: caramel mousse and ganache on shortbread, topped with a little white chocolate gingerbread man.
- **Popcorn Crafted Soda** at Ice Cold Hydraulics, with popcorn oat milk over Coca-Cola.
- **Holiday Dill-Light** at Tune-In Lounge, a gin cocktail with pickle juice and a chili-lime rim (21 and over, and definitely for pickle lovers).

There's also a new lounge for the event, the **Checkin' It Twice Lounge**.

## What's coming back?

The **Birria Taco** is back. Disney calls it the event's most popular dish since the first Jollywood Nights in 2023. You'll find it at **ABC Commissary**, along with a Street Corn Chicken Empanada and Arepas de Choclo.

## Is Jollywood Nights worth it for first-timers?

It depends on what you want from your trip. Here's how we'd think about it:

- **It's a great fit** if you love holiday food, you're adults or have older kids who'll stay up late, and you've already got a regular day at Hollywood Studios planned.
- **It may not be worth it** if you have little ones who fade after dinner, or if this is your only day at the park and you want to ride the big attractions. A regular park day plus a holiday treat may be the better value.
- **Remember the food costs extra.** Budget for the menu on top of the ticket. Splitting dishes is the easiest way to try more without spending a fortune.

**Our tip:** make a short list of three or four must-try items before you go, and map them by location. Hollywood Studios is spread out, and walking back and forth for snacks eats up a short evening fast.

## Where should you stay for Hollywood Studios?

If you'll be at Hollywood Studios late, staying close makes the trip back easy. The [BoardWalk Inn](/resorts/boardwalk-inn/) area has a walking path and boats to the park, and the [Riviera](/resorts/riviera/) sits on the Skyliner gondola line. Our [resort list](/resorts/) covers the rest.
