The holidays are one of the prettiest times to visit the Disneyland Resort in California, and the season is already underway. Disney shared its full holiday guide, so here are the highlights for first-timers, plus a hotel offer with a deadline coming up.

## When is the holiday season at Disneyland?

It runs **November 13, 2026, through January 6, 2027**, across both Disneyland park and Disney California Adventure.

## What are the highlights?

### At Disneyland park

- **Sleeping Beauty's Winter Castle**, covered in snowy decor and, Disney says, about 80,000 lights.
- **"it's a small world" Holiday**, with around 400,000 lights on the outside. It's lovely after dark.

- **Santa** has a new spot this year at **Mickey's Toontown Trolley Barn**.
- **Chip 'n' Dale's Ornament Trail** runs November 11 through January 6.

### At Disney California Adventure

- **Disney Festival of Holidays**, with food booths celebrating holiday traditions from around the world, plus **Disney ¡Viva Navidad!**, the festival's Latin holiday celebration.
- **World of Color - Season of Light**, the holiday version of the nighttime water show.
- A **Sip and Savor Pass** for the festival (six digital food coupons) is sold separately if you plan to graze.

### At the hotels

Santa also visits all three **Disneyland Resort hotels**, and holiday **character dining** at the hotels runs November 16 through January 3. Disney said reservations would open on Disneyland.com, so check there if it's on your list.

## Is there a holiday hotel deal?

Yes. Disney's offer is **up to 20% off select stays of 3 nights or more** at the [Disneyland Hotel](/disneyland/hotels/disneyland-hotel/), the [Grand Californian](/disneyland/hotels/grand-californian/), [Pixar Place](/disneyland/hotels/pixar-place/) and [The Villas at Disneyland Hotel](/disneyland/hotels/villas-at-disneyland-hotel/).

The fine print that matters:

- **Book by December 15, 2026.**
- **Stays from October 11 through December 18, 2026**, with travel finished by December 19.
- It's **subject to availability**, so not every room or date will qualify.

If you've been on the fence about a December trip, this is the window. Our [Disneyland Resort hotels page](/disneyland/) explains the differences between the three hotels in plain English.

## How should first-timers plan a holiday Disneyland trip?

- **Go on a weekday if you can.** Weekends in December are busy with locals.
- **See the lights at night.** The castle and "it's a small world" are both at their best after sunset, so save a little energy for the evening.
- **Plan one park per day for the evenings.** World of Color is in California Adventure and the castle lighting is in Disneyland, so split your nights.
- **Graze the festival for lunch.** The Festival of Holidays booths are an easy way to try a lot without a sit-down meal.
- **Book character dining as soon as it opens.** Holiday reservations go quickly.
