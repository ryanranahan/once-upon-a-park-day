Disneyland After Dark is Disneyland's series of themed, after-hours party nights, and the 2027 lineup is out. There's a brand-new night this year and a big Star Wars anniversary, so here's what you need to know.

## What are the 2027 Disneyland After Dark dates?

| Event | 2027 dates | Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| **Sweethearts' Nite** | Jan. 24, 26, 28, 31 and Feb. 2, 4, 7, 9, 11, 16 | General sale Dec. 10, 2026 |
| **Disneylandia Nite** (new) | Apr. 11, 13, 15 | Details due early 2027 |
| **Star Wars Nite** | Apr. 27, 29 and May 4, 6 | Details due early 2027 |
| **Pride Nite** | June 17 | Details due spring 2027 |

**Disneylandia Nite** is new for 2027. Disney describes it as a celebration of Hispanic and Latin culture, with characters from Coco and Encanto. **Star Wars Nite** lines up with Star Wars turning 50, and yes, one of the dates is May the 4th.

## What does an After Dark ticket include?

- **The event itself, 9 p.m. to 1 a.m.**, with entertainment, food and merchandise you won't find on a normal day.
- **Early entry to Disneyland park from 6 p.m.**, so you get a few hours before the party starts.
- **Unlimited digital PhotoPass downloads** from that night, using the QR code on your wristband in the Disneyland app.
- **A keepsake** and an event map.

Every After Dark night is a **separate ticket** from a regular park day, and it's open to all ages.

## When do tickets go on sale?

For **Sweethearts' Nite**, all at 9 a.m. Pacific time:

1. **Inspire Key holders:** Tuesday, December 8, 2026
2. **Magic Key holders:** Wednesday, December 9, 2026
3. **Everyone else:** Thursday, December 10, 2026

Disney says tickets are limited. Sale dates for the other three nights come out in early 2027 (Pride Nite in the spring).

## What's on at Sweethearts' Nite?

Disney lists a **Celebrate Love Cavalcade** on Main Street, a romantic castle show, **karaoke duets** at the Golden Horseshoe, a **Valentine's dance party** at Tomorrowland Terrace, jazz on the Mark Twain riverboat, and WALL-E and EVE projections on Space Mountain. There's a special menu at several spots, including heart-shaped ravioli and a "Love Duet" funnel cake, and a new merchandise collection.

## Is Disneyland After Dark worth it for first-timers?

We'd say it's worth it if:

- **You've already got a regular Disneyland day planned** and want a special night on top.
- **The theme matters to you.** A Star Wars fan at Star Wars Nite or a couple at Sweethearts' Nite gets the most out of it.
- **You're happy staying up late.** It ends at 1 a.m., which is a lot for little kids.

If it's your very first Disneyland visit and you only have one or two days, we'd spend them on regular park days first.

## Tips for the night

- **Be online right at 9 a.m. Pacific** on sale day, logged in to your Disney account.
- **Book table-service dining ahead** on Disneyland.com or in the app. Disney says there's a mobile walk-up list on the night, but spots are limited.
- **Use mobile ordering** for quick-service food.
- **Check the costume rules** before you dress up.
- **Stay close by.** Walking back to a [Disneyland Resort hotel](/disneyland/) at 1 a.m. beats a long drive. Our [Disneyland Hotel guide](/disneyland/hotels/disneyland-hotel/) is a good place to start.
