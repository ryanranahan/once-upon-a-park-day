A photo with Santa is one of those little moments families talk about for years. The good news for 2026 is that he's busy: Santa has a spot in **all four Walt Disney World theme parks and at Disney Springs**. Disney shared the full list, so here it is in one place, park by park.

## Where is Santa in each park?

| Where | Spot | Dates (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Animal Kingdom | Santa's Holiday Grove (new), at Theater in the Wild | Nov. 13 to Dec. 24 |
| Hollywood Studios | Walt Disney Presents | Through Dec. 24 |
| Magic Kingdom | Storybook Circus | Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving) to Dec. 24 |
| EPCOT | CommuniCore Hall | Nov. 27 to Dec. 24 |
| Disney Springs | Near Disney's Pin Traders (virtual queue) | Through Dec. 24 |

### Animal Kingdom: the new Santa's Holiday Grove

This one is new this year. It's at Theater in the Wild, across from the Finding Nemo show, and Disney says it has light snow flurries, holiday music, and its own treats. After Santa heads home on Christmas Eve, **Goofy dressed as Santa takes over from December 25 to January 3**, which is a sweet backup if your trip is over New Year's.

### Hollywood Studios: Walt Disney Presents

Santa is inside Walt Disney Presents, which also has a holiday display and a short program of classic Disney holiday cartoons. If you're going to [Disney Jollywood Nights](/blog/disney-jollywood-nights-food-2026/), keep an eye out for Duffy in a Santa suit too.

### Magic Kingdom: Storybook Circus

Santa arrives at Storybook Circus on **Thanksgiving Day, November 26**. He also rides at the end of Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade, so even if you don't meet him, the kids can wave.

### EPCOT: CommuniCore Hall

EPCOT's Santa starts **November 27**, the same day the [Festival of the Holidays](/blog/epcot-festival-of-the-holidays-2026/) begins. There's a gingerbread Spaceship Earth to look at nearby, and Goofy dressed as Santa is at The Odyssey.

### Disney Springs: book a spot in the app

Disney Springs works differently. Santa's workshop near Disney's Pin Traders uses a **virtual queue in the My Disney Experience app**, and there's no standby line. Disney says there are several times to join, so check the app on the day you go.

## How do you keep the Santa line short?

Disney doesn't publish wait times ahead of time, so here's what we'd do as first-timers:

- **Go early in the day or right after the parade crowds move on.** Lines for character visits tend to build mid-morning.
- **Pick the park you're already visiting.** It isn't worth crossing the resort just for Santa when he's in every park.
- **Visit earlier in the season if you can.** The week before Christmas is one of the busiest times of the year at Walt Disney World.
- **Have the kids ready with their "ask".** It sounds silly, but a little practice in line keeps a shy moment from turning into tears.
- **Look for a PhotoPass photographer.** Disney says they're at all four parks and Disney Springs, so you can be in the picture too.

## Is it worth meeting Santa at Disney?

If you have little ones who still believe, we'd say yes. Santa at a Disney park comes with the snowfall, the music and the decorations, and that makes the photo feel special. If your kids are older, a parade wave and a festival treat might be all you need.
