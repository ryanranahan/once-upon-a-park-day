If you're thinking about a Walt Disney World trip early in the new year, this one is worth knowing about. Disney has announced the dates and details for the **2027 EPCOT International Festival of the Arts**, and it's a milestone year: the festival's **10th**.

## When is the festival?

It runs **January 15 through March 1, 2027**. That's a nice window for first-timers, because January and February usually bring milder Florida weather than summer, and the festival gives EPCOT an extra layer of things to do.

## What is the Disney on Broadway Concert Series?

This is the headliner for a lot of people. Broadway performers sing Disney musical songs at the **America Gardens Theatre** in The American Adventure pavilion, with shows at **5:15, 6:30 and 7:45 p.m.** each night.

- **It's included with your EPCOT ticket.** Seats are first-come, first-served.
- **Want a guaranteed seat?** Disney sells dining packages that pair a meal at certain EPCOT restaurants with reserved seating. Disney says they go on sale **December 3**.
- **The performers change every two weeks.** Opening night (January 15) features Michael James Scott and Marisha Wallace. Later pairs include Ashley Brown and Josh Strickland, Adam Pascal and Sherie Rene Scott, Sierra Boggess and Norm Lewis, Kara Lindsay and Mason Reeves, and Caissie Levy and Ryan McCartan. Disney's site has the full schedule by date.

**Our tip:** if there's a performer you really want to see, check their dates first and plan your EPCOT day around it. If you don't want to pay for a dining package, plan to get in line well before the show time you want, especially on weekends.

## What's new for 2027?

This year's festival is presented by the **Disney Lorcana** trading card game, so there's a card-collecting thread running through the whole park:

- **A free quest:** pick up a Quest Booklet at Creations Shop (while supplies last), collect four quest cards around the festival, and trade them in for an exclusive card.
- **A scavenger hunt:** find the oversized festival cards for another exclusive card.
- **A limited six-card collector set** sold only at the festival, and a gift with purchase at select shops while supplies last.
- **New Lorcana photo spots**, chalk art, a paint-by-number mural, and card artists signing on select days.

You don't need to play Lorcana to enjoy the festival. The art, the food and the concerts work just fine on their own.

## What about the food?

Disney says there will be **19 Food Studios** around the park, serving small plates that are meant to look like art. Like EPCOT's other festivals, you pay item by item, so you can graze your way around World Showcase.

## Where should you stay for a Festival of the Arts trip?

If you'll head to EPCOT more than once, being close is a big help. The [Yacht Club](/resorts/yacht-club/), [Beach Club](/resorts/beach-club/) and [BoardWalk Inn](/resorts/boardwalk-inn/) are a short walk from the World Showcase entrance. For a lower price, [Caribbean Beach](/resorts/caribbean-beach/) and the other Skyliner resorts get you to EPCOT by gondola.
