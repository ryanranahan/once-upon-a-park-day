Disney Cruise Line is bundling Walt Disney World park tickets with select Florida sailings next spring and summer. The tickets are complimentary if you qualify, which is a real saving — but only if you were going to visit the parks anyway, and only after you read the limits.

The offer, as Disney describes it, is titled **Limited Time: Set Sail on Select Florida Cruises and Receive Walt Disney World Theme Park Tickets**. You can find eligible sailings by picking that special offer on [Disney Cruise Line's Find a Cruise page](https://disneycruise.disney.go.com/cruises-destinations/).

## How does the offer work?

Here are the rules, drawn from Disney's offer language:

- **Book by January 6, 2027.**
- **Sail on a select Florida departure between April and September 2027.** Not every sailing in that window qualifies, and Disney can change the list.
- On **select sailings of 4 nights or longer**, each guest ages 3 and older (age at the time of the cruise) gets a **2-day** Walt Disney World ticket.
- On **select 3-night sailings**, each guest 3 and older gets a **1-day** ticket.
- The tickets are valid for **one theme park per day**, and they're meant to be used **as part of your cruise vacation**.
- You have to make **theme park reservations in advance**. Disney says reservation inventory for this ticket is limited and subject to park capacity.
- **Children under 3** do not need a ticket or a park reservation.
- Tickets are delivered to the **My Disney Experience** account tied to the primary guest's email, within **20 days** of the cruise departure.
- Eligible staterooms are categories **04A through 11C**. **Concierge staterooms and suites are not eligible.**
- It applies to **new bookings and existing ones** that meet the rules. To add it to a cruise you already have, **call the Disney Cruise Line Contact Center**.
- You **cannot combine** it with other offers or discounts, including the Guaranteed Stateroom with Restrictions offer and Walt Disney World offers or discounts.
- Disney Vacation Club points can be used to book an eligible sailing.
- Taxes, fees, port expenses, gratuities, and Port Adventures are not included. The park tickets have no cash value and are nontransferable.

A [planDisney panelist](https://plandisney.disney.go.com/question/buy-cruise-option-includes-parks-offers-know-applies-647980/) confirmed that the **May 12–16, 2027, 4-night Bahamian cruise on the Disney Fantasy** from Port Canaveral is an eligible sailing, with the same stateroom limits.

## What are the tickets actually worth?

The tickets themselves are free. The honest way to value them is to look up what Disney is charging for the same kind of ticket on the days you'd use them.

I checked [Disney's official theme park ticket calendar](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/admission/tickets/theme-parks/) on **November 23, 2026**, for start dates from **April 1 through September 30, 2027** — the same months as the cruise offer. Prices below are for standard **1-park or 1-park-per-day tickets**, before tax. Disney adds **6.5% sales tax** at purchase. I did not include Park Hopper, because the offer says one park per day.

### If you're on a 3-night cruise: a 1-day ticket

A 1-day, 1-park adult ticket (ages 10+) in that window is **$119 to $219** before tax, depending on the park and the date:

| Park | Lowest adult price | Highest adult price |
|---|---|---|
| Disney's Animal Kingdom | $119 | $189 |
| EPCOT | $129 | $204 |
| Disney's Hollywood Studios | $139 | $209 |
| Magic Kingdom | $139 | $219 |

The $119 days are in mid to late August and early September, at Animal Kingdom (including August 17, August 23, and September 1). The $219 days are April 16–18 at Magic Kingdom. With tax, that range is **$126.74 to $233.24** per adult.

Child tickets (ages 3–9) in the same window run **$114 to $214** before tax, again depending on the park and date.

### If you're on a 4-night cruise or longer: a 2-day ticket

A 2-day, 1-park-per-day adult ticket is **$258.06 to $388.47** before tax, depending on the start date:

- **Least expensive start date in the window:** August 23, 2027, at **$258.06** before tax ($274.83 with tax).
- **Most expensive start date in the window:** April 15, 2027, at **$388.47** before tax ($413.72 with tax).

Child 2-day tickets for those same start dates are **$248.71** and **$378.89** before tax.

A concrete example: if you sailed the Fantasy on May 12–16 and used a 2-day ticket starting May 12, Disney's calendar prices that ticket at **$359.95** before tax for an adult ($383.35 with tax), and **$350.44** before tax for a child ($373.22 with tax). For two adults and two children ages 3–9, that's **$1,420.78** before tax, or **$1,513.14** with tax — if you would have bought those tickets anyway.

Your number will be different. Price the actual park days you're considering on [Disney's calendar](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/admission/tickets/theme-parks/), because the start date changes the cost.

## When is this a good deal?

It's a good deal if you already wanted a few park days next to a Florida cruise, and your stateroom category qualifies.

It's less compelling if:

- You were only going to cruise, and now you'd have to add hotel nights, dining, and transportation to use the tickets.
- You wanted Park Hopper, Lightning Lane, or a longer ticket. Those cost extra, and this offer doesn't replace them.
- You're in a concierge room or suite.
- You were hoping to stack it with a room or ticket discount. You can't.

Also remember the reservations. A free ticket doesn't help if you can't get a park reservation for the day you want. Book those as soon as the tickets show up in My Disney Experience.
