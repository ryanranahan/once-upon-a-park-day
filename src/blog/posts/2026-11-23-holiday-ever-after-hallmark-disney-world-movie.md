A holiday movie set at Walt Disney World? That's our kind of cozy night in. Hallmark's first Disney World holiday film arrives this weekend, and Disney celebrated with a red-carpet premiere at Disney Springs. Here's when to watch, and what to do if it gives you the urge to book a trip.

## When and where can you watch it?

- **Hallmark Channel:** premieres **Saturday, November 28, 2026**.
- **Disney+ and Hulu:** streaming from **Sunday, November 29, 2026**.

## What's it about?

**Lacey Chabert** stars as Lindsey, whose family vacation at Walt Disney World runs into the family of someone she used to date. A wish at the **Cinderella Wishing Well** sets things in motion. Disney says **Jodi Benson**, the original voice of Ariel, has a cameo, so keep your eyes open.

## Can you visit the places from the movie?

The Cinderella Wishing Well is a real spot at Magic Kingdom, near Cinderella Castle, and it's free to visit with your park ticket. It's a quiet corner that a lot of first-timers walk right past, so it's worth a detour for a photo (and a wish).

## How do you plan your own Disney World holiday trip?

If the movie has you daydreaming, here's what's happening at Walt Disney World this season, with our guides to each:

- **Santa is in all four parks and Disney Springs** through December 24. Here's [where to find him, park by park](/blog/where-to-meet-santa-disney-world-2026/).
- **EPCOT's Festival of the Holidays** runs November 27 to December 30, with 20 Holiday Kitchens and the Candlelight Processional. Our [first-timer's guide](/blog/epcot-festival-of-the-holidays-2026/) has the plan.
- **Disney Jollywood Nights** at Hollywood Studios is a separately ticketed holiday evening with a big menu. See [what to eat and drink](/blog/disney-jollywood-nights-food-2026/).
- **Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade** rolls through Magic Kingdom, with Santa riding at the end.

## Is there still time to book for this December?

Maybe, but go in with flexible expectations. The weeks around Christmas and New Year's are some of the busiest of the year, so rooms and dining reservations get tight. A few tips:

- **Early December is usually calmer** than the week of Christmas.
- **Be flexible on the resort.** Our [resort pages](/resorts/) explain every option, from value resorts to deluxe hotels like the [Grand Floridian](/resorts/grand-floridian/), which is known for its holiday gingerbread display.
- **Or plan ahead for next year.** Watching the movie with a notebook is a perfectly good way to start.

Happy watching, and happy planning.
