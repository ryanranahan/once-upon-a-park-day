A Disney cruise around the holidays is a special kind of trip: warm weather, a Christmas tree in the lobby, and Santa at sea. Disney calls these **Very Merrytime Cruises**, and they've just shared a full guide for the 2026 and 2027 seasons. Here's what first-timers need to know.

## What is a Very Merrytime Cruise?

It's the holiday theme Disney Cruise Line adds to **select sailings in November and December**. Not every sailing in those months has it, so when you search Disney Cruise Line's site, look for itineraries marked **Very Merrytime**.

## Where do Very Merrytime Cruises sail from?

For the 2026 and 2027 holiday seasons, Disney lists four home ports:

| Port | What Disney lists |
|---|---|
| **Fort Lauderdale** | 3- to 7-night Bahamas trips and 7-night Eastern Caribbean trips in 2026. 4-night Bahamas and a 7-night Eastern Caribbean trip in 2027. |
| **Port Canaveral** | 3- to 5-night Bahamas trips plus 5- and 7-night Western Caribbean and a 7-night Eastern Caribbean trip in 2026. Two 4-night Bahamas trips in 2027. |
| **Galveston** | 4- and 5-night Western Caribbean trips (Progreso, Cozumel) in 2026 and 2027. |
| **San Diego** | 4-night Baja trips and 5- to 7-night Mexican Riviera trips in 2026. A 5-night Baja and a 7-night Mexican Riviera trip in 2027. |

Many of the Bahamas trips stop at one or both of Disney's own islands, Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point. Ports vary by sailing, so check each itinerary.

## What happens on board?

Disney's list of holiday extras includes:

- **A 24-foot Christmas tree** and holiday decor around the ship.
- **Mickey & Minnie's Merrytime Tree Lighting.**
- **Mickey & Minnie's Holiday Party** on the upper deck.
- **Season's Greetings with Santa**, with a chance to meet Santa and do holiday crafts.
- **Characters in holiday outfits.**
- **Holiday treats**, with special sweets like gelato and cupcakes on the Dream and Wish class ships, plus seasonal coffees and lounge snacks.
- **New holiday photo backdrops** themed to The Muppet Christmas Carol and The Nightmare Before Christmas.

## Can you decorate your stateroom?

Yes, for a price. Disney sells **Onboard Gifts & Amenities** before you sail, and the holiday options include room decor, treats and a small "Nautical Tree". They're extra-cost add-ons you order ahead of time.

## How do you pick the right Very Merrytime sailing?

Here's how we'd narrow it down as first-timers:

1. **Start with the port you can reach.** Driving to the port saves a flight and a hotel night, which matters a lot in the busy holiday season.
2. **Choose the length next.** A 3- or 4-night Bahamas trip is a great first cruise. Longer Caribbean trips mean more ports and more sea days.
3. **Decide whether Christmas week matters.** Sailings right around Christmas and New Year's are usually in the highest demand, so compare prices with other dates. Early December sailings still have the decor and the Santa visit.
4. **Pick the ship.** Our [ship comparison](/cruises/) explains the differences in plain English. The [Disney Dream](/cruises/disney-dream/) and [Disney Wish](/cruises/disney-wish/) are where Disney says the holiday gelatos and cupcakes are served.
5. **Book early.** Holiday sailings are popular with families who have school breaks.
