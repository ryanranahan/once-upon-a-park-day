The first handmade candy canes of the Disneyland season land this weekend. If that's on your list, you need a plan before you walk through the gates, because these sell out and the line starts early.

Disney changed the 2026 dates after the first announcement, so use the updated schedule below. The process itself is the same one they used last year: wristbands, not a mobile waitlist.

## What's the plan for November 27–29?

Pick the park that matches the date. You can't get Friday's cane in California Adventure, and you can't get the weekend canes on Main Street.

- **Friday, November 27:** [Disneyland Park](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/), at **Candy Palace and Candy Kitchen** on Main Street, U.S.A.
- **Saturday, November 28, and Sunday, November 29:** Disney California Adventure, at **Trolley Treats** on Buena Vista Street.

That's the opening weekend. If your trip is later in December, the rest of the dates are at the bottom of this post.

## How do the wristbands work?

Disney's own description is short and strict:

- Wristbands are used for a chance to buy **one candy cane**.
- The canes are **not available through mobile order**.
- A **very limited number** are available each release date, while supplies last.
- You have to buy **in person**.
- **Limit one per guest per day.**
- **No discounts** apply, and there are **no exchanges or refunds**.
- Dates and prices can change.

When you get a band, it should have a **return time** printed on it. You come back to the **same shop** at that time to actually buy the cane. That's the part people miss: getting a wristband is not the same as getting the candy cane. Build the rest of your day around the return window.

## How should you line up this weekend?

I haven't stood in this year's line yet, so this is the practical version of what usually works, not a trip report.

1. **Get to the entrance early.** Plenty of people arrive about an hour before the published park opening. Check that day's hours the night before on [Disneyland's calendar](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/calendars/).
2. **Once you're in, go straight to the shop.** Don't detour for photos or coffee first. Cast Members will usually direct you into a line outside the store.
3. **Keep your whole party together.** The limit is one cane per guest, so everyone who wants one needs to be in line.
4. **Stay in line even if it looks long.** Quantities are limited, but the end of the line is not always the cutoff people think it is.
5. **After you get a band, look at the return time immediately.** Put it in your phone. Be back at the shop on time, not 20 minutes late.
6. **Don't run.** It's against park rules, and you don't need to if you arrive early enough.

You'll need valid admission for that park. These are not sold in Downtown Disney or at the hotels.

## How much do they cost?

Disney has not published a 2026 price that I can verify. Last year, people were paying about $24 for an 18-inch, 5-ounce cane. Treat that as a ballpark from last season, and confirm the price at the shop this year.

## When are the rest of the 2026 dates?

After this weekend, the canes keep rotating between the two parks. This is the updated schedule reported after Disney changed the original dates:

**Candy Palace and Candy Kitchen, Disneyland Park:** November 27 and 30; December 2, 7, 9, 14, 16, 21, 23, and 25.

**Trolley Treats, Disney California Adventure:** November 28 and 29; December 1, 6, 8, 13, 15, 20, 22, and 24.

Christmas Eve is in California Adventure. Christmas Day is in Disneyland Park. Later weekdays in December are often a little less frantic than opening weekend, but "less frantic" is still a line.
