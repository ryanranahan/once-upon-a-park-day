## The short answer

- **Pick a parks trip** if your family wants rides, big days, and the chance to see as much as possible.
- **Pick a cruise** if you want Disney magic at a slower pace, with meals, entertainment and kids clubs mostly taken care of.
- **Do both** if you're flying into Orlando anyway. Port Canaveral is close to Walt Disney World, so a few park days plus a short Bahamas cruise is an easy combination.

## How they compare

| | Parks trip | Disney cruise |
|---|---|---|
| **Pace** | Busy. Early mornings, lots of walking, a plan for each day | Relaxed. Unpack once and let the ship do the work |
| **Planning** | More of it: park tickets, dining reservations, Lightning Lane | Less of it: activities, port adventures and specialty dining |
| **Food** | You pay for each meal or snack (or add a dining plan) | Main dining, quick service and room service are included in the fare |
| **Kids** | Together all day, unless you split up | Free kids clubs for ages 3 to 17 give everyone some downtime |
| **Getting around** | Buses, boats, Skyliner, monorail or a car | Your hotel moves with you |
| **Best for** | Ride lovers and first-timers who want the classic park experience | Multi-generation groups, couples, and families who want an easier trip |

## Questions to ask yourselves

- **How much walking can everyone handle?** Park days can mean many miles on foot. A cruise is much easier on little legs and grandparents.
- **Do you want to plan, or just show up?** A parks trip rewards planning. A cruise is closer to all-inclusive.
- **What's the must-do?** If it's a specific ride or land, that's a parks trip. If it's a character dinner, a beach day and a show every night, a cruise covers it.
- **How long do you have?** Short Bahamas cruises run 3 or 4 nights. A good first parks trip is usually four or five park days.

## Doing both

Disney's shortest cruises leave from Port Canaveral and Fort Lauderdale. Starting with a few park days and ending with a short cruise gives you the excitement first and the rest at the end.
