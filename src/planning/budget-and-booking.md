Prices change constantly, so we don't quote them here. Use this list to build your budget, then price each line on Disney's site for your dates.

## The big costs

1. **Where you stay.** The biggest choice. Value resorts cost the least, and Deluxe resorts the most. See **[Value, Moderate or Deluxe?](/resorts/value-moderate-deluxe/)**
2. **Park tickets.** Price depends on how many days you buy and which dates. Longer tickets cost less per day.
3. **Food.** Quick-service meals, table-service meals and snacks add up fast. Character meals cost more but double as entertainment.
4. **Lightning Lane.** Optional, but popular. Multi Pass and Single Pass are priced separately.
5. **Getting there.** Flights or gas, plus a car or transfer if you need one.
6. **Extras.** Souvenirs, photos, special events and tips.

## How booking and paying works

- **Book a package** (room plus tickets) or the room only. Packages bundle everything into one reservation.
- For **2027 packages booked more than 30 days out**, a **$200 deposit** is due within 3 days of booking. The **balance is due 30 days before arrival**.
- Booking within 30 days of arrival means paying in full at booking.
- Cancelling 2 to 29 days out costs **$200**. At 1 day or less, the full package is non-refundable. Always check the terms for your own booking.

## Ways to save

- **Travel in quieter seasons.** Prices for rooms and tickets drop outside school holidays.
- **Stay at a Value or Moderate resort** and spend the difference on experiences.
- **Buy more ticket days.** The per-day price falls the longer your ticket.
- **Share meals and snacks.** Quick-service portions are often big.
- **Book a garden or standard view.** At Deluxe resorts, views cost extra, and you won't spend much time in the room.
- **Watch for Disney's offers** for room discounts. The **[Deals and news](/news/)** page keeps track.

## Budgeting for a cruise

A Disney cruise fare already includes your meals, room service, kids clubs and shows, so the budget looks different. Plan for gratuities, drinks, port adventures and specialty dining. See **[Your first Disney cruise](/cruises/first-cruise/)**.
