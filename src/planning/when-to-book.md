## 6 to 12 months out: book your hotel

Disney suggests booking your hotel **6 to 12 months ahead**. Popular resorts and holiday weeks fill up first, so earlier is better if you have your heart set on one place.

If you book a **2027 Disney vacation package** more than 30 days out, a **$200 deposit** is due within 3 days of booking, and the rest is due **30 days before arrival**. Check the terms for your own booking, since they can change.

## 60 days out: dining reservations

Everyone can book dining reservations **60 days ahead**. If you're staying at a Disney Resort hotel, you can book on the 60-day mark **for your whole stay, up to 10 nights**, all at once. That's a big head start for popular restaurants and character meals.

## 30 days out: final payment

Package balances are due **30 days before arrival**. This is also a good time to link your tickets and reservations in the My Disney Experience app.

## 7 days out: Lightning Lane (Disney hotel guests)

Guests at Disney Resort hotels, and a few others like the Swan and Dolphin, can buy **Lightning Lane Multi Pass and Single Pass 7 days before their stay**, for up to 14 days of their trip. Sales open at **7:00 AM Eastern**.

## 3 days out: Lightning Lane (everyone else)

If you're not at a Disney hotel, you can buy Lightning Lane **3 days before your park day**.

## Every morning: Early Theme Park Entry

Disney Resort guests get into every park **30 minutes early**, every day of their stay. Deluxe and Deluxe Villa guests also get **Extended Evening Hours** on select nights. Disney says both continue through 2027.

## Cruising?

Disney Cruise Line opens activity, Palo, spa and port adventure bookings on a schedule based on your Castaway Club level. First-time cruisers book last, so set a reminder. See **[Your first Disney cruise](/cruises/first-cruise/)**.
