## First-hand when we can

Once Upon A Park Day is written by Ryan, who has stayed at many Walt Disney World resorts and sailed on the Disney Magic, Dream and Fantasy. When a guide says "I" or "my," it's about something Ryan has actually done.

## Researched guides are labeled

Some guides cover places we haven't stayed yet. Those pages carry a **Researched guide** badge near the top. They're built from public sources and written in our own words, and we add first-hand notes and photos after a visit.

## Where the facts come from

- **Official sources first.** Disney's, Universal's and Disney Cruise Line's own websites and announcements come first.
- **Trusted fan sites second.** When an official page doesn't say, we check well-known planning sites and compare them. If they disagree, we say so or leave the detail out.
- **No guessing.** If we can't confirm something, we don't publish it as fact. Anything still being checked is marked on the page.
- **No invented prices.** Prices change constantly, so we point you to the official booking page instead of quoting numbers we can't stand behind.

## Keeping it current

Every guide shows when its facts were last checked. Hours, menus, rides, refurbishments and itineraries change often, so always confirm the details with Disney, Universal or Disney Cruise Line before you book.

## Photos

Photos on this site are Ryan's own unless a caption says otherwise. Until we've visited everywhere, some resort, hotel and ship pages use openly licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited under each photo and on our [photo credits](/about/photo-credits/) page. We never use Disney or Universal's own press photos. Where we don't have any photo yet, you'll see a "Photos coming soon" placeholder.

## Independence

Once Upon A Park Day is an independent site. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The Walt Disney Company or Universal Orlando Resort.
