## Disney press photos — hold until PR says yes

Ryan pointed to the Walt Disney World News Room ([disneyexperiences.com/disneyworld-press/](https://disneyexperiences.com/disneyworld-press/)) as a source we can pull from, with credit. Ryan’s OneDrive photos stay the priority and were not replaced.

The News Room’s [Supplemental Terms of Use](https://disneyexperiences.com/supplemental-terms-of-use/) are stricter than a simple credit line. In short:

- Use is limited to **editorial news use** by authorized press / journalists who have registered.
- Keep copyright and other proprietary notices intact, and **do not modify** the materials. Resizing to WebP or cropping would conflict with that “do not modify” rule.
- Don’t use photos out of context, and don’t use them in a way that suggests an association with Disney products, services, or brands.
- Authors and publishers who want photography for **travel guidebooks** — including “electronic guidebooks available on the Internet or as software applications” — **are not authorized users**, may not use the content, and **must contact the public relations team**. The media guide lists Walt Disney World PR at `WDW.Public.Relations@disney.com`.
- High-resolution files are delivered through Brandfolder after a press login. Preview JPEGs appear on some asset pages, but the same terms still apply.

Because this site is an independent travel guide, **no Disney press images were added to the repo or the preview**. If PR clears guidebook use (and, ideally, a web-resized credit version), the credit format on those pages should follow the press captions: **`Photo: Disney`**, or **`Photo: Disney (Kent Phillips, photographer)`** when a photographer is named. Press pages usually put the name in parentheses at the end of the caption, for example “(Harrison Cooney, Photographer)” or “(Disney)”.

Until then, the empty slots below stay placeholders for Ryan’s own photos.
